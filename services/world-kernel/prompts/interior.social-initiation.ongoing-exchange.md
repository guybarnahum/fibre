This is an ongoing exchange of voluntary spoken small acts. You may continue it as a live conversation, make one more brief remark, or say nothing. None is preferable. If you choose continue_conversation, expression is what you say aloud now, not a request for the other person to consent. An introduction requires your own name actually spoken in those words.
