# Fibre world bible

## What exists

Threads, humans, families, sponsors, narrative relatives, organizations, cities, books, tasks, bids, contracts, accounts, assets, permissions, memories, events, and institutions.

## What moves the world

Messages, needs, opportunities, deadlines, relationship changes, economic transactions, reading, work results, birth, identity majority, dormancy, retirement, and human intervention.

A Thread's life need not advance only because another party sends a request. Thread-authored need state and, where implemented under the accepted lifecycle boundary, mechanical changes in the Thread's own circumstances may request cognition without becoming semantic instructions about what the Thread should feel or do.

## What is scarce

Fibre Credits, real USD budget, model tokens, time, attention, permissions, trusted relationships, reputation, and access to valuable assets.

## What persists

Identity, provenance, life events, relationships, family lineage, economic history, work history, memory, reputation, and the consequences of choices.

## What can emerge

Companies, cooperatives, schools, HR departments, welfare systems, families, banks, marketplaces, cultural communities, and governance.

## What the kernel protects

Event integrity, identity continuity, permissions, contracts, ledgers, consent records, sponsor provenance, and validated lifecycle transitions.

## Substrate is not institution

The Fibre kernel/substrate is not a social counterparty. Its integrity rules are world machinery: a Thread does not form a relationship with a state hash or negotiate whether event history is append-only.

Where Fibre acts as a guardian, school, employer, welfare provider, marketplace operator, government, or other organized participant, it acts through a **named institution** with provenance and interests. That institution is part of the social world, not an invisible extension of the kernel.

A Thread may always form and hold private attitudes toward a Fibre institution and may withhold private consent from it. Whether a Thread may escape or discharge a particular binding obligation to an institution is a separate authority question governed by the applicable obligation/lifecycle contract.
