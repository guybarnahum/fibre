## Identity assertion and admission boundary

The first Slice-A hostile review found that registry-version pinning did not also freeze **claim-admission discipline**, and that a natural-language atomicity checker detected punctuation patterns rather than single propositions. Its two S1 and six S2 findings blocked further bulk authoring until corrected.

The corrected admission contract separates `admission.policy` (source/provenance authority) from immutable `admission.claimDiscipline` (claim-shape authority). Current `normalizeIdentityAssertion()` and historical `rehydrateIdentityAssertion()` must not reinterpret old rows under a new policy. New v2 assertions require structural `claimPredicate: {subject, predicate, object}` plus a prose backstop; the first named discipline is `identity_atomic_material_proposition:1`. Earlier v1 history remains immutable and may not serve as a bulk-authoring bypass.

The assertion digest binds the pinned registry version, revisions cannot change that version, and mixed/v2 identity views bind only the domain definitions actually used rather than drifting whenever unrelated registry domains change. Unsupported historical versions fail before destructive schema repair. Culture, ancestry, lineage, geography and embodiment remain **context-only** at this milestone; no derived causal standing is inferred from them.

See the current identity contract in [Thread Passport & Identity Provenance](../../architecture/thread-passport-identity-provenance.md). Exact review chronology and older validators remain in Git history.

## Autobiography and situated evidence

- Lived culture/language, family and place claims require same-Thread admitted witnesses; false current claims may disappear from a view without erasing historical assertions.
- Historical facts and autobiographical memories have separate truth authorities. Memory revisions retain the same subject, preserve supporting and contradicting evidence, and are anchored externally in Thread history **without endorsing the remembered interpretation**. Neither a later reinterpretation nor truncation may silently erase lineage evidence.
- At #38, recall timestamps and Thread-self-authored memory were not earned by the schema or projection alone.
- Slice C's narrow hostile rereview returned **CLEAR** at `a3f61bc2d3356f9fae3145f189464500d3939a10`.

## Memory visual companion

Every newly admitted first memory revision creates a durable `MEMORY MOMENT` visual-companion obligation in the **same transaction**. Its first remembered meaning supplies the prompt authority; later reinterpretation cannot silently rewrite that historical prompt. Generation failure leaves an explicit outstanding obligation, and missing cached media may reopen it without changing the evidence or synthetic/captured truth.

Generated synthetic reconstruction must never be relabeled as a captured historical photograph. The photo-completion worker remains a simple obligation loop, not a new queue framework. Slice D was re-attacked and cleared after fixes at `399377fc24f41a154b080fa931ea0c4bdddb417b`. A future real captured photograph may need a distinct companion lineage alongside the synthetic reconstruction; that feature was **not** admitted by #38.
