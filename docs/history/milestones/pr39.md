### D1 — distinguishability, not causal particularity

Raw and explicit-entity-normalized attribution were both `5/5` majority-correct. Because each target was evaluated as a separate stateless five-way task, the scorer's Bernoulli chance model used `p=0.2` per target and `0.2^5 = 0.00032` for five successes.

Permanent interpretation:

> A blind rater could reliably distinguish and attribute the five life/self-account texts, including after explicit entity normalization. Surviving environmental and cultural cues mean this is not evidence of Fibre-level historical particularity or causal non-interchangeability by itself.

Raw and normalized conditions are not independent confirmations. Future testing needs harder decoys and failure controls rather than another ceiling-only result.
