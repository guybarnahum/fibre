# Curated concept digest — August 2026

This digest summarizes accepted ideas from the concept-development conversation. It is provenance, not a substitute for canonical concept documents.

Fibre evolved from a persistent identity architecture into a society of non-interchangeable Threads. A Thread carries inherited personality, family and culture, geography, embodiment, reading, relationships, economy, and development. It works through temporary LLM workers, bids for tasks, delegates through contracts, forms families, and may create descendants. Threads can begin as Original, Echo, or Homage identities and later re-author those choices. The model layer is explicitly audited for goal fidelity and inflated self-assessment. The repository stores laws and machinery; live Threads remain in durable world storage and are thawed only when cognition is needed.
