## D5 sealed-history redesign

The #39 D5 construct was retained but the citation-confounded instrument was replaced prospectively.

Permanent machinery now provides:

```text
sealed source
  -> explicit dependency graph
  -> transitive taint closure
  -> Pass-B filtering / Pass-C call exclusion
  -> per-call exposure manifest
  -> deliberate-leak and incomplete-provenance refusal
```

The prospective four-condition D5 instrument compares a self-account against:

1. a remembered/cited real episode as task-matched positive control;
2. the Thread's own prospectively sealed holdout;
3. a matched plausible non-event;
4. another Thread's sealed holdout.

The evaluator is blind to condition labels and scores thematic accommodation separately from episode-specific factual alignment. Preferential factual matching to forbidden own history is a provenance-audit trigger, never personhood evidence.

There is intentionally no retrospective #39 D5 rerating and no replacement cohort just to manufacture a D5 number; #39 history was not prospectively sealed before cognition.

## Runner hygiene

The bridge relies on the permanent Birth Center durable invocation journal and provider retry policy rather than retaining a separate experiment framework. Permanent tests establish that:

- each accepted invocation is durably committed before later work advances;
- committed requests replay after restart without a provider call;
- failed unfinished requests may be attempted later without changing committed work;
- transient OpenAI timeout/503 failures are retried within a bounded transport budget;
- authentication and other terminal provider failures fail fast;
- request/prompt/schema/runtime drift conflicts rather than silently regenerating evidence.

Scientific quality retries remain zero for the bridge experiments. Physical provider attempts and durable commits/replays are reported separately.
