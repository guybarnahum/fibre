# ADR-0027 — A natural repertoire of small social acts

**Owner decision for review:** A salient encounter with a co-present person
offers a choice among `none | acknowledge | greet | remark |
invite_conversation`. Cognition decides whether to act, what is expressed
and its private immediate Experience. Salience and World presence are
opportunities, not a demand to communicate. There is no target rate for
acknowledgment, greeting, acceptance or meeting.

A chosen nod, greeting or remark is a World-owned objective Encounter Story.
The actor's private Experience is distinct from the other person's
independent noticed/not-noticed attention. Only a noticed act can produce
one ordinary, claimed observed social opportunity for the recipient.
An invitation, unlike a small act, remains subject to the addressed
Thread's independent accept/decline/defer decision before conversation.
The existing one-hour opportunity freshness, life-debt and witnessed
co-presence checks remain in effect.

## Computation and authority

Four act-triggered recipient opportunities **per unordered pair per
continuous shared venue visit** is a bounded execution limit, not a
psychological or social etiquette rule. The shared visit starts at the
later of the two continuously evidenced World venue-arrival intervals,
found through a bounded previous-situation walk. When the start cannot be
verified within the bound, no further act-triggered opportunity is issued.
The existing observed-opportunity table records whether a chance arose
from arrival or a small act; a venue/time indexed count is performed only
after a small act was admitted and the recipient noticed. There is no
per-person tick, periodic partner scan or second social engine.

## Vision and scope

- **Behaviourally causal:** the independently authored act changes
  objective World history, the actor's immediate Experience and, when
  noticed, the recipient's future opportunity to act.
- **Context-only:** the venue's admitted affordances are available to the
  social mind; the source implementation does not prove they change its
  choices.
- **Operational:** the four-opportunity cap controls computational exposure,
  not Thread temperament, social drive or conversational length.
- **Deferred:** greeting satisfaction of Body needs, introduction/name
  learning, reconsideration beyond the source-only event triggers now implemented,
  retained
  autobiographical meaning bending future social choice, long-term
  relationship formation and human visitor integration. These continue
  through World Story → personal Experience → selective Memory → later
  salience and judgment. None is rejected as an ambition.
- **No new identity/relationship fields:** the act's choice consumes the
  existing individual context; no model outcome is converted to an
  automatic friendship, relationship, reward or obligation. World records
  the observable event; other people's attention remains their own.
- **Temporary bound:** at most 16 previous situations are traversed to
  prove visit continuity, and four reply opportunities per pair are
  admitted within it. Both can be revisited using measured runtime and
  lived-visit evidence without changing the domain meaning of social acts.
  There is no new permanent constraint or closed constitutional path.

The design deliberately declines two alternatives: forcing a conversation
to make every act social, and adding a repeated social-initiative timer.
Either would substitute orchestration for ordinary voluntary life. The
remaining cost is one extra attention call per enacted small act, and one
ordinary social decision call only if a noticed recipient opportunity
later reaches cognition. Salience remains mechanical, private action
remains self-authored, and objective Story remains separate from private
Experience.

**Evidence boundary:** tests can verify the causal World and attention
routing and the one-visit limit with fixture cognition; they cannot
prove autonomous interest, a preferred distribution of acts, social
need satisfaction, or a later relationship change. Local validation
and staging behaviour must be reported separately before marking this
decision accepted or upgrading a public claim.
