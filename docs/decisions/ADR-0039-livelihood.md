# ADR-0039 — Livelihood: what a Thread can do, what it does with its life, and what money does to it

**Accepted by Guy, November 21, 2026**, with the livelihood and information slice plan (L1–L6, I1–I3). It realises slice 4 of [interests, felt expertise, livelihood and social standing](../concepts/interests-expertise-and-livelihood.md) and draws on the [economy](../concepts/economy-and-fibre-credits.md) and [task marketplace](../concepts/task-marketplace.md) concepts. It is written with L1 and extended by each later slice.

## Why

On staging (November 18–21) all eight Threads lived one life: 22 years old, in Addis, with two caregivers and a sibling, none studying, working or looking for work, every day spent at one library on one shared inquiry. The live World admitted only learning places from Genesis. Their interests, books and conversation had converged on testing claims against evidence, and in a day of about 110 acts of regard not one was a slight: there was nothing different enough to rub against.

## Decision

**A livelihood is the Thread's own, never assigned.**
- **Skills** are what a Thread has evidence of being able to do, in its own words, each citing the memories (later the work, courses and moments) it rests on. There is no level and no score; a buyer's judgement is the buyer's.
- **Its life situation** — what it is doing with its life now, where, its rhythm, hopes, worries and open questions — is its own continuation of its own past, in its own words.
- Both are formed once from its formative memories, family, interests and the places it knows, and are later revised only in its own reflection (L5).

**World attests facts, the Thread decides.** World keeps places, where a Thread actually was (attendance), and later the ledger and prices. Whether to go to work today, to learn, apply, accept, quit or borrow is always the Thread's own choice, with consequences.

**No money urge (Guy).** The wish to earn is an emergent outcome of the Body's existing regulators. Money's absence or presence reaches the Body only as anticipation from one provision fact, through what it blocks or makes possible (energy conservation, blocked wants as frustration, standing, refusals, closure for debts, attachment and reciprocity, remorse, shelter). The concept's earlier "resources need" is replaced by this.

**Work is real (Guy).** Paid work is real-world work done by the Thread's own cognition and tools, never simulated; Fibre is its payroll and bank (L3).

**A bigger city.** The live World's admission of Genesis places now admits a school and a market beside learning places. The admission's version names the identity namespace of every admitted place and stays fixed, so each place keeps its reference. A workplace or school a Thread names is a place it discovers when it first goes there, by the same name, city and country.

## Consequences

- **Causal status (L1):** skills and situation are **Context-only** — they reach the plan, social attention, conversation and contact as the Thread's own evidence, and no acceptance criterion yet requires them to change a choice. Attendance is a World fact read by operators only. They become behaviourally causal with L2 (work lived) and L4 (money felt).
- **Dignity is emergent (Guy):** an external offer of work is appraised by the social Body like any experience. This changes the wording of the invariant "every externally initiated request must pass a dignity appraisal"; the change is recorded here when L3b builds it.
- No permanent constraint. Employers as persistent World actors, caregivers as live Threads, skill fading without use, and inheritance across generations are deferred, each with its extension path in the design.
