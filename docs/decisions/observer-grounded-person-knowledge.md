# Observer-grounded person knowledge and curiosity

**Owner decisions (October 22, 2026):**

1. **Body drive must match the stimulus.** Social-contact pressure may make any co-present person worth considering, while exploration pressure matters socially when the observer has actually noticed a *new, unfamiliar* person. Stillness alone is not an invitation to approach every stranger. The exploration drive remains available in private regulation, interoception and environmental attention, without compelling speech.
2. **A person is not born knowing everyone's name.** World arrival Stories describe objective arrival and the observable physical presence of captured witnesses. Social Situated Percept may give the observer the counterpart's name only with an existing lived relation or a prior admitted accepted-and-spoken encounter; otherwise the description uses authorized public embodiment and activity, or simply "a person." Physical appearance never implies nationality, culture, religion, motives, personality or worth.

These are observer-relative projections, not new mutable World identities. They preserve future introductions, learning names from speech, recognized faces, degrees of acquaintance and context-dependent attention as **deferred** mechanisms, rather than pretending the current relationship gate models them. The underlying Body's sustained-low-novelty proxy is likewise provisional; real novelty, engagement and contact-window quality belong to the deferred Body-needs work.

**Vision/ambition guard:** An arriver's noticed Experience is **behaviourally causal** for a later salience gate; the description/known-name fields are **context-only** until differential social judgment is demonstrated; the novelty-matched exploration anchor is **behaviourally causal** for gating, although its Body evidence remains provisional. Objective Story and public embodiment remain separate authorities from inner experience and observer knowledge. No permanent limitation, second social engine, demographic shortcut or synthetic meeting quota is introduced. Source tests cannot establish autonomous relationship development, richer motivation, learned introductions, or increased meeting rates; those paths remain open via genuine Experience → retained Memory → later encounter and relationship evidence.
