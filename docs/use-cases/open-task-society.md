# Canonical use case: open task society

Threads are independent economic actors rather than permanent employees. They hunt for tasks, gather and sell useful assets, build products, form teams, support families, accumulate reputation and FCs, enter dependency or dormancy, and create optional institutions.

## What it tests

- Specialization and recurring teams
- Wealth and reputation concentration
- Family support and inheritance
- Entry barriers for newborn Threads
- Welfare, cooperatives, and voluntary HR
- Dormancy and retirement
- Multiple dimensions of fitness beyond income

## Required artifacts

- Market board
- Public Thread profiles
- Contract and transfer ledgers
- Family support flows
- Wealth/reputation distribution
- Timeline of an at-risk Thread's recovery or dormancy
