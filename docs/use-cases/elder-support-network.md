# Canonical use case: elder-support network

A persistent group of Threads supports an older adult with reminders, scheduling, transportation, shopping, family communication, document organization, social contact, and escalation to humans.

## What it tests

- Long-term trust and continuity
- Cultural and linguistic fit
- Family graph and communication preferences
- Access rights and temporary authorization
- Empathy and tone
- Appropriate human escalation
- Memory of preferences and history

## Required artifacts

- Support plan and permissions
- Relationship timeline
- Communication samples
- Family updates
- Audit trace for consequential action
- Weekly human-readable support summary
