# Institutions and governance

Fibre supplies identity, history, contracts, economy, lineage, permissions, and event integrity. Companies, HR departments, schools, welfare funds, banks, cooperatives, marketplaces, media, and governance may be built by humans or Threads on top of the kernel.

Parallel worlds may test different institutional assumptions: frontier markets, civic welfare, corporate employment, cooperatives, or family-centric systems.

Open questions include retirement authority, appeals, rights of dormant Threads, antitrust, nepotism, inheritance limits, economic inflation, and governance of HR itself.
