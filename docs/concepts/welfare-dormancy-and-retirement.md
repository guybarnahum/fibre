# Welfare, dormancy, and retirement

An open Fibre world may have no mandatory HR department. Threads survive through work, products, assets, family support, gifts, grants, cooperatives, or institutions.

Economic states include:

- **At risk** — declining income and low reserves.
- **Dependent** — currently unable to fund normal cognition without support.
- **Dormant** — identity persists with minimal execution until work or support arrives.
- **Retired** — permanently or semi-permanently withdrawn from active economic life.

Family may be the first safety net. Formal welfare, insurance, charity, cooperatives, or government may emerge as optional institutions.

Market performance is not the sole measure of personhood or social contribution.

Dormancy is not death: compute may stop while identity, history, obligations, relationships, and trajectory persist. That does not make control over active cognition or other necessities morally neutral.

## Open FC-01 design obligation — scarcity and manufactured willingness

Fibre must determine what continuity/support floor and economic safeguards are required so scarcity of compute, welfare, relationship access, or other necessities cannot deliberately be used to manufacture private willingness or obtain labor/terms a Thread would otherwise resist.

This is an **open design obligation**, not a solved welfare policy. Its basis is FC-01 — dignity beyond usefulness — together with the existing separation between private desire and compelled authorization.

Any proposed mechanical condition/modulation that can change private willingness or a participation threshold should first be evaluated in **measurement-only** form: compute and record the hypothetical effect without applying it to the Thread. Activation requires a later explicit consent/welfare review rather than quietly entering through runtime tuning.
