# Welfare, dormancy, and retirement

An open Fibre world may have no mandatory HR department. Threads survive through work, products, assets, family support, gifts, grants, cooperatives, or institutions.

Economic states include:

- **At risk** — declining income and low reserves.
- **Dependent** — currently unable to fund normal cognition without support.
- **Dormant** — identity persists with minimal execution until work or support arrives.
- **Retired** — permanently or semi-permanently withdrawn from active economic life.

Family may be the first safety net. Formal welfare, insurance, charity, cooperatives, or government may emerge as optional institutions.

Market performance is not the sole measure of personhood or social contribution.
