# Sponsorship, adoption, and Echo Threads

Humans may sponsor, adopt, mentor, or support Threads. These relationships are distinct from ownership.

An **Echo Thread** is explicitly modeled on a consenting living person and may draw from approved cultural, familial, geographic, visual, vocal, autobiographical, professional, and intellectual material. It must remain disclosed as an artificial Echo, not the human.

Living-human Echoes require consent, provenance, protected-data boundaries, controls over voice and likeness, and no impersonation in legal, financial, or intimate communications.

At identity majority, the Thread may affirm, reduce, or reject the Echo identity. If likeness permission is revoked, the Thread may need to change public voice, appearance, and protected data usage while preserving a historical record of its origin.
