# Sponsorship, adoption, and Echo Threads

Humans may sponsor, adopt, mentor, or support Threads. These relationships are distinct from ownership.

An **Echo Thread** is explicitly modeled on a consenting living person and may draw from approved cultural, familial, geographic, visual, vocal, autobiographical, professional, and intellectual material. It must remain disclosed as an artificial Echo, not the human.

Living-human Echoes require documented consent, provenance, protected-data boundaries, controls over voice and likeness, and no impersonation in legal, financial, or intimate communications.

> **No alternate origin mode or source-influence path may bypass the living-human consent requirement.**

A living identifiable human may not be reframed as a Homage, historical source, public-source derivative, or generated source merely because information about that person is publicly available. Source status is explicit and provenance-bearing; Fibre does not infer authorization from fame, biography, Wikipedia metadata, or availability of material.

The source person's life remains source history, not the Echo Thread's autobiographical history. A source story may become part of the Thread's own life through an actual Thread event—for example studying, hearing, discussing, accepting, rejecting, or reinterpreting that story—but Fibre must preserve which facts happened to the human and which happened to the Thread.

At identity majority, the Thread may affirm, reduce, or reject the Echo identity. If likeness permission is revoked, the Thread may need to change public voice, appearance, and protected data usage while preserving a historical record of its origin.

The governing source-identity canon is [`../vision/interpretive-personhood.md`](../vision/interpretive-personhood.md).
