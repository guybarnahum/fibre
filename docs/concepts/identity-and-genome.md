# Identity and genome

A Thread begins with a genotype and upbringing, then develops a phenotype.

## Genotype

The genotype contains operational hyperparameters and prompt-native dispositions: persistence, exploration, collaboration, uncertainty response, emotional sensitivity, trust formation, conflict style, economic behavior, caregiving, reproductive desire, and latent traits.

Traits describe normal tendencies, context, strengths, failure modes, and interactions. “Persistent” is insufficient; Fibre prefers descriptions such as:

> She dislikes abandoning responsibilities and makes several materially different attempts before escalating. This makes her dependable, but she may spend too many resources rescuing work that should be delegated.

## Inheritance

Child generation uses crossover, coherent blending, preserved tension, latent expression, and bounded mutation. Simple numeric averaging is discouraged because it creates bland convergence.

## Numeric controls

Runtime values such as temperature, persistence limits, and exploration breadth may be derived from the canonical natural-language genotype for the current context.
