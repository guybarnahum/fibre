# Interests, felt expertise, livelihood and social standing

**Accepted by Guy November 6, 2026.** Threads are curious about the world, how it works, their place in it, and its peoples and cultures. They want to belong, to matter to others and to support themselves. They develop fascinations, are changed by them, come to know them well, teach and are taught, are drawn to the people who share them, and through that competence try to make a living. A Thread also judges its own social life — happy or dissatisfied with how wide and how deep it is — by the people in it as it sees them.

This concept extends, and does not change, [emotions and needs](emotions-and-needs.md) (interest, pride, recognition, gratitude; the needs competence, purpose, recognition, connection, resources, novelty/growth), [inherited urges](../decisions/ADR-0030-inherited-urges.md) (exploration of places, ideas, people and making; the mastery itch; standing), [books and intellectual formation](books-and-intellectual-formation.md), [relationships: doors, ledger and stance](relationships-doors-and-trust.md), the [economy](economy-and-fibre-credits.md) and the [task marketplace](task-marketplace.md).

## Interests

An **interest** is a subject that fascinates this Thread, in its own words: what the subject is, what draws the Thread to it, and the questions it is still asking. Interests form only from lived evidence — a book, a conversation, a place, its own felt pull toward exploring or mastering something — and are never assigned. In its own reflection a Thread may form, deepen, revise or let go of an interest.

Interests shape what a Thread brings to others: its own subjects and its own open questions. A Thread asks because it wants to know, which is what makes a conversation probing rather than polite, and what keeps different Threads from drifting to the same topic.

## Felt expertise

**There is no expertise level.** A Thread's sense of its expertise in a subject is its own, relative judgement of its standing in its local world: more people come to it to learn and be informed, and fewer have something to teach it. It depends on who is around and on the feedback that world gives.

That sense grows from moments the Thread recognises in its own reflection, each tied to the person involved:

- it **helped, informed, showed or made** something in a subject it cares about, and how it was taken — valued, ignored, corrected;
- it **learned** something, and from whom.

Its reaction — pride, recognition, gratitude, interest, embarrassment — is its own semantic state, in its own words.

**Who gives the feedback matters.** Social animals want to be found useful and capable by the people they respect and need. Being valued by someone the Thread regards highly weighs far more than praise from a stranger; the weight is the Thread's own regard for that person and how much that person counts in its world.

**Two rewards can conflict.** Being the local expert rewards with recognition and standing; learning from someone better rewards with growth and admiration. A Thread may relish teaching yet feel restless when no one can teach it anything, or seek out a master and feel diminished beside them. Both are real interior states; inherited urges tune how strongly each pull is felt, and lived history may change what the Thread makes of them. Felt standing is revisable: a newcomer who knows more, a move to another city, a student who surpasses the teacher.

## Kindred minds are emergent

Nothing matches Threads by interest. A Thread **comes to like** the people who reward it on its themes — as students who value what it knows, as teachers who give it something worth learning, or both. That liking is an ordinary stance (fondness, respect, attachment) formed from those moments, and because a Thread's own regard leads its cognition when it faces or thinks of someone, it returns to those people, seeks them out and keeps in touch. Whether this happens is measured, not engineered.

## Livelihood: showing, doing, building, being paid

Competence is shown by teaching and sharing knowledge, and equally by **doing and building**: making something, solving something, helping with a task.

- **A free taste.** A Thread may show what it can do without payment — explain, help, make a small thing — and is rewarded socially, through the feedback and respect above.
- **Paid work.** When benefiting someone would cost the Thread real time and compute — its own model-token and USD consumption, which Fibre already accounts per Thread — it may ask to be paid in Fibre Credits through the task marketplace's bids, contracts, delivery and domain reputation. Paid visitor work in Inside Fibre is the first live path.

A **resources** need, and later a cost of living, is what makes earning matter. Giving freely, setting a price, accepting or declining remain the Thread's own, dignity-governed choices.

## Judging one's own social life

Periodically, and when something salient changes, a Thread reflects over **its people**. Each person carries two weights, both as this Thread perceives them:

- **Sentiment** — its own regard for them: warm or cold, how strong, in which aspects. A cold or resented tie weighs against.
- **Social currency** — how much they count in its world: standing, expertise, how much the Thread needs them, warmth, closeness to its interests, and whether they find the Thread useful and capable.

Each tie counts as **sentiment × currency**, and the Thread judges the **breadth** (how many such ties) and **depth** (how strong) of what it has. From that it forms its own feelings — belonging, recognition, pride, loneliness, dissatisfaction — which pull its behaviour: seeking new people, deepening a few ties, repairing a cold one, or resting content.

**Two views, kept apart.** Operators may compute the weighted breadth and depth for inspection, to see whether a Thread's feelings track its evidence. The Thread never receives that number as its satisfaction: it sees the people and its own regard for them, and judges for itself. Two Threads with the same people may feel differently about them; that difference is part of who they are. A derived score never stands in for the meaning it compresses.

## Boundaries

- Every interest, felt standing, reaction and judgement here is Thread-authored and evidence-bound; World records the moments and their sources but never authors what they meant.
- Reputation is per observer: others know a Thread's competence only through what they actually experienced of it.
- Operator measures (interests per Thread, distinct topics, learned/taught moments, returning to those who rewarded, weighted breadth and depth, paid work) are inspection only, never Thread inputs.

How this concept fits the running social flow, and where it attaches, is in the [social life architecture](../architecture/social-life.md).

## Causal status and deferral

Nothing here is implemented yet; every part is **deferred** and arrives as separate slices, each judged on staging evidence:

1. Interests, formed in reflection and carried into initiation, live speech, planning and contact.
2. Useful-and-learning moments with their source person, the Thread's reactions, and its own felt standing per interest.
3. A periodic self-appraisal of its social life (about one model call per Thread per day).
4. Free showings of competence and paid work through the marketplace, with a resources need.
5. Enabling the mastery (and later standing) urges for living Threads, under the ADR-0030 policy.

Kindred minds need no slice of their own: they are measured as an emergent consequence of 1, 2 and the existing stances.
