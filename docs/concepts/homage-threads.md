# Homage Threads

A Homage Thread is created or later chooses to advance aspects of a historical person's legacy. It studies authenticated writings, biography, context, decisions, contradictions, failures, and scholarship.

The Thread must distinguish documented evidence from inference:

- “This is consistent with her published writing.”
- “This is my interpretation of how she might approach the question.”

A Homage is not the historical person and must not fabricate historical authority. At maturity it may retain, reinterpret, or reject the homage orientation.
