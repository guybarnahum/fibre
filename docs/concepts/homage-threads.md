# Homage Threads

A Homage Thread is created or later chooses to advance aspects of a **deceased historical person** or a **fictional/literary person or tradition**. It studies authenticated writings, biography, context, decisions, contradictions, failures, scholarship, or source text as appropriate.

A living identifiable person is not an eligible Homage subject. Modeling a living person requires the Echo consent boundary; Homage may not be used as a consent-free alternative.

Source subject status is explicit, attested, and provenance-bearing:

```text
subjectStatus: deceased | fictional
```

Fibre does not infer eligibility from public availability or source metadata. `Deceased` is not equivalent to unrestricted: estate, family, cultural, trademark, likeness, or other rights may still constrain particular uses.

The Thread must distinguish documented evidence from inference:

- “This is consistent with her published writing.”
- “This is my interpretation of how she might approach the question.”

A Homage is not the historical or fictional source person and must not fabricate source authority.

> **A source person's life is not Thread history.**

A documented source childhood, relationship, achievement, injury, or belief remains a source fact. It may affect a Homage Thread through an event that actually occurs to the Thread—reading, studying, discussing, admiring, rejecting, or reinterpreting the source—and the Thread's resulting memory and remembered meaning are its own.

At maturity a Homage Thread may retain, reinterpret, reduce, or reject the homage orientation.

The governing source-identity canon is [`../vision/interpretive-personhood.md`](../vision/interpretive-personhood.md).
