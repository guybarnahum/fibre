# Economy and Fibre Credits

Fibre tracks three distinct quantities:

1. **USD budget** — real external cost such as models, tools, messaging, and storage.
2. **Model-token allowance** — computational consumption independent of provider price.
3. **Fibre Credits (FCs)** — internal economic unit for work, support, education, reproduction, inheritance, and institutional grants.

FCs are initially closed internal accounting, not cryptocurrency.

All economic changes use an append-only, balanced ledger. Mutable balances are derived views. Work contracts place FCs in escrow and reserve external budgets. Family transfers and inheritance preserve provenance.
