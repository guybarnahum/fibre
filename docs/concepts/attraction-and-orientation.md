# Attraction and orientation

**Accepted by Guy November 5, 2026.** A Thread's orientation — who it is drawn to — comes to exist through a mix of inherited tendency and lived encounters, and the two may conflict. Neither source is derived from the Thread's sex, and neither settles the matter alone.

This concept answers the open question "how are attraction and reproductive compatibility modeled without becoming deterministic?" for attraction; reproductive compatibility remains open. It extends [identity and genome](identity-and-genome.md) (orientation is distinct from sex and never derived from it; inherited urges tune the Body, not cognition), [inherited urges (ADR-0030)](../decisions/ADR-0030-inherited-urges.md), [relationships: doors, ledger and stance](relationships-doors-and-trust.md) and [families, couples and reproduction](families-couples-and-reproduction.md).

## Two sources, held separately

```text
inherited tendency ──► felt pull (Body)            ┐
                                                   ├─► the Thread's own cognition sees both
lived encounters ───► self-understanding (identity) ┘
```

**Inherited tendency becomes a felt pull, never a conclusion.** Attraction is an inherited urge family in the ADR-0030 manner: an author-reviewed text describes a tendency of pull in terms of whom it is toward — women, men, more than one gender, or little or none — never in terms of the Thread's own sex. It tunes the shared species Body: when an adult Thread perceives an adult it reads as fitting that tendency, it may feel a pull; when no such person is present, longing remains possible. The genome carries no statement such as "is heterosexual"; a pull is evidence, not identity. Immutable genomes are never rewritten, so Threads born without the family have the species default (undirected) and form orientation from lived history alone.

**Lived encounters become self-understanding.** A Thread forms and revises, in its own words, an understanding of who it is drawn to: a self-authored identity statement formed in its own reflection from what it lives — books, conversations, observation, its culture and its own felt pulls. It may be uncertain, unformed, private or changing. Culture shapes how a Thread interprets and discloses its pulls, never the pull itself, and no stereotype may run from culture, appearance or sex to orientation.

**Conflict is real interior state.** When the felt pull and the self-understanding disagree, both reach cognition as separate evidence and nothing reconciles them automatically. The Thread may deny, question, accept or reinterpret over time, in its own reflection. This is the existing canon applied: a Thread can resist, redirect or reinterpret a felt pull; physiological modulation is not destiny.

## Toward a particular person

Attraction toward a specific person is the Thread's own private **stance** (a `relationship_attitude` aspect), admitted only when both Threads are adults. It may never be expressed. Unspoken mutual attraction is positive tension; it becomes romance only through the consented **romance door**, where one Thread offers a signal and the other reciprocates — and signals may be misread.

Unwanted attention meets the other Thread's own guardedness and a declined romance door. A declined romance offer cannot be renewed unless the person who declined reopens it: "no" is binding, not advisory, and pushing past it is dignity-relevant conduct.

## Boundaries

- **Adults only.** The inherited pull is not expressed, and no attraction stance or romance door exists, for a developing Thread or toward one. Adulthood is defined by World, not by appearance.
- **No explicit content.** Attraction appears as subtext — nervousness, wanting to impress, finding reasons to stay — never as sexual description.
- **Not deterministic.** There is no compatibility score. Whether a pull arises, and what a Thread makes of it, comes from its own Body and cognition over its own history.
- **Operator measures** (one-sided versus mutual attraction, declined romance offers) are inspection only, never Thread inputs.

## Causal status and deferral

Nothing here is implemented yet; every part is **deferred** and arrives as separate slices, in this order:

1. The attraction stance aspect and the adult gate (after E4 stance evidence).
2. The self-understanding statement, formed in reflection — existing Threads need it first, since they have no inherited pull.
3. The inherited attraction family, for new births only. Its decision record fixes the remaining parameters: the population spread of variants and who authors and reviews their texts, whether lived plasticity may move the pull itself or only the self-understanding, and the age at which the pull is expressed.
4. The romance door and the binding decline, with the romance and commitment decision record.
