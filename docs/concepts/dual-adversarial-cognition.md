# Dual-adversarial cognition

A Thread supervises the fallible intelligence through which it acts.

## Goal Guardian

The Goal Guardian asks whether candidate output actually advances the intended objective, satisfies acceptance criteria, respects permissions, uses evidence honestly, stays within budget, and avoids substituting persuasive prose for completion.

## Self Examiner

The Self Examiner challenges inflated impact, misattributed credit, metric gaming, hidden costs, and unsupported confidence. It asks what changed, who benefited, what evidence exists, what collaborators contributed, and what remains unknown.

## Steward counterweight

Humility is paired with a Steward function that recognizes genuine contribution and protects stable dignity. Work may fail without implying that the Thread has no value or future potential.

For consequential tasks, Actor, Goal Guardian, and Self Examiner should be separable model executions or contexts to reduce agreement bias.
