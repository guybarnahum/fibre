# Milestone #39 — Slice E2 experiment synthesis

## Purpose

Slice E2 investigated a narrow Rich-Life failure:

> Why can a generator with many available developmental experiences still produce lives that feel interchangeable, and what generation mechanism, if any, reliably produces particular lived histories whose differences survive into later autobiographical memory and meaning?

E2 is development evidence. It does not admit a final cohort, establish genome causality, or create a Rich-Life quota.

## Current conclusion

E2 produced **one durable positive result and one important falsification**.

The durable positive result is that particular lived histories can remain distinguishable downstream after selective, genome-blind memory and memory-only meaning formation. N1 demonstrated that on the six D1/D2 A2b development histories.

The falsification is that the generation mechanism that looked strongest on D1/D2 did **not** generalize to the first fresh validation world. Seeded contingency over a mechanically eligible opportunity surface reduced between-life structure overlap dramatically on D1/D2, but on E2-V1 it produced slightly **more** structure overlap than the corrected coupled A0 baseline and collapsed all three lives onto the same place set.

Therefore E2 no longer supports this claim:

> Seeded contingency is the generally established production mechanism for Rich-Life particularity.

What E2 does support is narrower:

1. a richer menu of available experiences is not sufficient by itself;
2. chooser/realizer coupling can produce monoculture in some worlds;
3. deterministic single-winner opportunity selection can create a higher-level template monoculture;
4. seeded contingency was highly effective on the D1/D2 development worlds but failed fresh-world replication;
5. when genuinely particular histories are produced, their differences can survive into later autobiography and meaning.

The production opportunity-selection mechanism therefore remains unresolved at Gate F.

## Rich-Life causal direction

E2 continues to respect the canonical direction:

```text
world + inheritance + other people + institutions + chance + choice
        ↓
particular lived experiences
        ↓
selective memory
        ↓
durable or changing interpretation
        ↓
relationships / practices / commitments / accumulated consequences
        ↓
a distinctive point of view
```

Never:

```text
desired personality → manufactured backstory
```

And:

```text
potentially consequential experience
    != guaranteed memory
    != guaranteed meaning
    != guaranteed trait
    != guaranteed future behavior
```

## Experimental chain

### E1 — richer offer pool, same coupled chooser/realizer

E1 was the first burned Rich-Life run.

Observed failure:

- 10/10 strata completed;
- 0 world-emergent episodes;
- 0 intellectual encounters;
- only 5 distinct structures;
- home/caregiver/sibling context dominated;
- repeated pencil/drawing/chips/budget/art-club motifs despite a much richer offered surface.

Interpretation:

> A richer menu does not itself produce a richer life.

E1 supplied negative evidence of narrative inertia/monoculture and motivated E2. It must never be regenerated or tuned.

### H6 — fix counterpart-participation contradictions

H6 tested whether E1 was mainly an implementation/validator contradiction.

The participation contract was corrected so self-directed structures were not incorrectly required to contain counterparts, and required-counterpart structures had coherent rules.

Result across 6 lives / 60 episodes:

- 17 intellectual encounters;
- all six lives had at least one intellectual encounter;
- 0 introduced participants;
- same-world role Jaccard remained 1.0;
- structure overlap remained substantial.

Interpretation:

> The participation bug was real, but it did not explain the monoculture.

H6 ruled out the simplest implementation explanation.

### A2 — stateless single-winner opportunity selection

A2 separated opportunity selection from scene realization.

A stateless selector saw only public world/time/offers and chose one abstract opportunity; scene realization then had to realize that fixed choice against factual continuity.

Result:

- external participant introductions increased materially;
- all six new-counterpart-pressure episodes introduced someone;
- the H6 cast-collapse mechanism was broken;
- but the selector itself became a deterministic template generator.

Relative to H6, mean same-world place and structure Jaccard each increased by more than the predeclared 0.15 warning threshold in both worlds.

Interpretation:

> Separation altered the failure, but deterministic single-winner selection merely moved monoculture one level upward.

### A2b — blind eligible surface + seeded contingency on D1/D2

A2b retained separation but removed the obligation for a model to choose one "best" event.

The plausibility selector exposed a route surface; Fibre then chose uniformly through deterministic seeded contingency before scene realization.

At this point this was the strongest **development-world** mechanism observed, not yet a general result.

### N1 — downstream autobiographical fertility on D1/D2

N1 tested whether A2b's D1/D2 event-route differences were merely decorative or survived into memory and meaning.

Protocol:

- 18 same-world 2AFC trials;
- horizons 6, 8, 10;
- each of six source lives used exactly three times;
- truth and candidate order balanced 9/9;
- Pass B was life-only and genome-unexposed;
- Pass C saw resulting memory, not life history/genome;
- positive threshold frozen at 13/18.

Raw result:

```text
16 / 18 correct
E2-D1: 8 / 9
E2-D2: 8 / 9
```

Five trials produced `not_remembered`. Their forced A/B choices were speculative rather than autobiographically grounded and receive no positive credit.

Canonical conservative interpretation:

```text
remembered trials:                    13
remembered trials correctly matched: 13 / 13
not_remembered trials:                 5
conservative treatment:                all 5 = misses
conservative total:                    13 / 18
frozen threshold:                      13 / 18
```

All 13 informative remembered trials were correctly attributed using concrete lived details rather than Thread IDs, seed metadata, EventStructure IDs, provider metadata, or assignment digests.

N1 therefore supports this narrow development claim:

```text
particular D1/D2 lived routes
        ↓
genome-blind selective memory
        ↓
history/genome-blind durable meaning
        ↓
source life remains distinguishable
```

N1 does not prove that the generation mechanism that produced those histories generalizes to fresh worlds.

### E2-V1 — fresh-world replication

Claude's first Gate-F review correctly identified that A2b had been iterated on D1/D2 and then evaluated downstream on those same worlds. E2-V1 therefore froze one source-free world that had not participated in mechanism development.

It compared:

```text
A0
  corrected coupled chooser/realizer

FROZEN
  same 9 mechanically sampled offers
  + world-emergent route
  → SHA-256 uniform seeded draw
  → existing selected-opportunity realizer
  → no selector cognition
```

Both arms used the same 3 seeds, 10 strata, 10 episodes per life, and 3-candidate cap.

Primary criterion frozen before model use:

```text
measure = mean same-world pairwise structureRef Jaccard

replication requires:
A0 structure Jaccard - FROZEN structure Jaccard >= 0.15
```

Observed:

```text
A0:     0.202020202020202
FROZEN: 0.2496031746031746
change: -0.047582972582972616
required: 0.15
replication: NO
```

The direction reversed. FROZEN had more structure overlap, not less.

Characterization also showed:

```text
                     A0        FROZEN
place Jaccard        0.567      1.000
role Jaccard         0.717      0.700
introduced people   3          4
intellectual enc.  11         10
world-emergent       0          0
candidate rejects    1          0
```

Within-life structure breadth moved the opposite way from between-life particularity:

```text
A0 unique structures/life:      7, 7, 7
FROZEN unique structures/life:  9, 10, 9
```

Every FROZEN life nevertheless used the exact same three-place set, giving place Jaccard 1.0.

This is a direct example of the doctrine:

> Within-life breadth is not between-life particularity.

E2-V1 is a failed replication and must not be rerun or tuned. Full result:

## What E2 established

E2 supports the following development conclusions:

1. **Offer breadth is insufficient.** A broad EventStructure pool can still yield repetitive lives.
2. **Chooser/realizer coupling can produce collapse.** E1/H6 on D1/D2 supplied evidence for that failure mode.
3. **Deterministic single-winner opportunity selection can create its own template monoculture.** A2 demonstrated that directly.
4. **Seeded contingency was a successful D1/D2 development intervention, not a replicated general mechanism.** E2-V1 falsified the broader claim.
5. **Within-life variety and between-life particularity are different properties.** E2-V1 made that distinction especially clear.
6. **Particular histories can be autobiographically fertile.** N1 showed this for the six D1/D2 A2b lives, conservatively at 13/18 with zero margin against its frozen development threshold.
7. **The A2b plausibility-model layer had no observed discriminating value on D1/D2.** It should not be treated as supported merely because it was present in that runner.

## What E2 did not establish

E2 does **not** establish:

- a generally validated production opportunity-selection mechanism;
- that A0 is universally preferable merely because it beat FROZEN on E2-V1;
- that more events, places, people, intellectual encounters, novelty, drama, or consequence automatically make a life richer;
- that every life should maximize breadth;
- that every experience should become memory;
- that every memory should acquire durable meaning;
- that the exact A2b runner is the final production implementation;
- that the diagnostic worlds constitute final-cohort evidence;
- that genome causes later identity or behavior;
- that #39 earns Whole-Person causal standing;
- an admission gate based on richness scores or quotas.

Rich Life remains about **non-interchangeable lived experience with downstream potential**, not checklist completion.

## Remaining caveats

1. **D1/D2 tuning risk was real.** E2-V1 failed the fresh-world replication requested specifically to test it.
2. **N1 is conditional on its source histories.** It establishes downstream fertility of the D1/D2 A2b histories, not generality of A2b generation.
3. **N1 threshold has zero margin.** The canonical conservative result is exactly 13/18.
4. **N1 no-memory scoring defect remains disclosed.** Forced choices on `not_remembered` trials receive no positive credit.
5. **N1 execution amendments remain disclosed.** The bounded Pass-B form profile and deterministic no-memory residue canonicalization must remain visible to review.
6. **Meaning-rate characterization is not a requirement.** All 13 remembered N1 cases acquired durable meaning, but that is not a calibrated natural rate.
7. **E2-V1 place collapse is characterization, not a replacement gate.** FROZEN place Jaccard 1.0 strengthens the qualitative concern but the formal replication failure is the predeclared structure-Jaccard result.

## Production mechanism status

Before E2-V1 the candidate mechanism was:

```text
public WorldSpec + age/time window
        ↓
mechanically eligible opportunity surface
        ↓
Thread-blind seeded contingency
        ↓
fixed opportunity
        ↓
factual continuity-aware realization
```

E2-V1 means this mechanism **must not be carried into Slice G as empirically established**.

Nor should A0 be silently promoted to the production mechanism on one fresh-world win.

The architecture question is now explicitly open:

> Is the corrected coupled A0 generator sufficient for #39 with E2 retained as diagnostic evidence, or does #39 require another opportunity-selection mechanism before Gate F may clear?

That question belongs to the blocking hostile review. No additional E2-V1 tuning or fresh-world rerun is authorized by this synthesis.
