# Milestone #39 — Slice E2 experiment synthesis

## Purpose

Slice E2 investigated a narrow Rich-Life failure:

> Why can a generator with many available developmental experiences still produce lives that feel interchangeable, and what generation mechanism produces particular lived histories whose differences survive into later autobiographical memory and meaning?

E2 is development evidence. It does not admit a final cohort, establish genome causality, or create a Rich-Life quota.

## Plain-English conclusion

E2 found that **having a richer menu of possible experiences is not enough**.

When the same cognition chooses both what happens and how to fit it into the person's current life, it tends to choose experiences that are easy to ground in already-known people and places. That creates biographies with different wording but similar underlying lives.

The useful mechanism was to separate those responsibilities:

```text
mechanically eligible opportunities
        ↓
blind seeded contingency chooses what happens
        ↓
factual continuity constrains how it happens
        ↓
particular lived history
        ↓
selective memory
        ↓
possible durable meaning
```

Under that mechanism, the diagnostic lives stopped collapsing onto one common event skeleton. A downstream blind test then found that, whenever an autobiographical memory actually formed, the resulting memory/meaning bundle remained distinguishable from another life in every observed informative trial.

The evidence therefore supports **particular lived routes with real downstream autobiographical fertility**, not event-count maximization or artificial diversity targets.

## Experimental chain

### E1 — richer offer pool, same coupled chooser/realizer

E1 was the first burned rich-life run.

Observed failure:

- 10/10 strata completed;
- 0 world-emergent episodes;
- 0 intellectual encounters;
- only 5 distinct structures;
- home/caregiver/sibling context dominated;
- repeated pencil/drawing/chips/budget/art-club motifs despite a much richer offered surface.

Interpretation:

> A richer menu does not itself produce a richer life.

E1 supplied negative evidence of narrative inertia/monoculture and motivated the E2 mechanism investigation. It must never be regenerated or tuned.

### H6 — fix counterpart-participation contradictions

H6 tested whether E1 was mainly an implementation/validator contradiction.

The participation contract was corrected so self-directed structures were not incorrectly required to contain counterparts, and required-counterpart structures had coherent rules.

Result across 6 lives / 60 episodes:

- 17 intellectual encounters;
- all six lives had at least one intellectual encounter;
- 0 introduced participants;
- same-world role Jaccard remained 1.0;
- structure overlap remained substantial.

Interpretation:

> The participation bug was real, but it did not explain the monoculture.

H6 ruled out the simplest explanation and strengthened the selection/realization-coupling hypothesis.

### A2 — stateless single-winner opportunity selection

A2 separated opportunity selection from scene realization.

A stateless selector saw only the public world/time/offers and chose one abstract opportunity; scene realization then had to realize that fixed choice against factual continuity.

Result:

- external participant introductions increased materially;
- all six new-counterpart-pressure episodes introduced someone;
- the H6 cast-collapse mechanism was broken;
- but the selector became a deterministic template generator.

Relative to H6, mean same-world place and structure Jaccard each increased by more than the predeclared 0.15 warning threshold in both worlds.

Interpretation:

> Separation was useful, but a deterministic single-winner selector merely moved the collapse one level upward: different scenes, same abstract opportunity skeleton.

A2 therefore supported the H2 coupling diagnosis while rejecting the exact deterministic selector as a production mechanism.

### A2b — blind eligible surface + seeded contingency

A2b retained separation but removed the obligation for a model to choose the one "best" event.

The selector exposed a plausible route surface; Fibre then chose uniformly through deterministic seeded contingency before scene realization.

In the completed development artifact:

```text
worlds:                    2
lives / world:             3
episodes / life:           10
admitted episodes:         60
candidate attempts:        8
rejected attempts:         2
mean structures / life:    8.17
introduced participants:   10
intellectual encounters:   23 / 60
world-emergent episodes:    6 / 60
```

Mean same-world Jaccard:

```text
                    H6       A2       A2b
D1 place           0.833    1.000     0.833
D1 role            1.000    0.633     0.833
D1 structure       0.313    0.576     0.092

D2 place           0.639    1.000     0.639
D2 role            1.000    0.733     0.667
D2 structure       0.410    0.643     0.114
```

A2b removed A2's deterministic structure-template collapse while preserving useful cast differentiation.

Important diagnostic finding:

```text
all-offers-plausible windows = 60 / 60
mean plausible routes/window = 10.00
```

The plausibility cognition therefore added no discrimination on D1/D2. The useful mechanism actually exercised was:

```text
blind mechanically eligible opportunity surface
        ↓
frozen seeded contingency
        ↓
selected opportunity
        ↓
factual scene realization
```

A production design should not retain the plausibility-model call merely because it appeared in this experiment; it needs independent justification if kept.

### N1 — downstream autobiographical fertility

A2b established event-route particularity. N1 tested whether those differences were merely decorative or survived into memory and meaning.

Protocol:

- 18 same-world 2AFC trials;
- horizons 6, 8, 10;
- each of six source lives used exactly three times;
- truth and candidate order balanced 9/9;
- Pass B was `life_only` and genome-unexposed;
- Pass C saw the resulting memory, not life history/genome;
- positive threshold frozen at 13/18.

Raw result:

```text
16 / 18 correct
E2-D1: 8 / 9
E2-D2: 8 / 9
```

However five trials produced `not_remembered`. The rater was still forced to choose A/B and its rationales on those five trials were speculative rather than autobiographically grounded. Their lucky guesses are not positive evidence.

Load-bearing interpretation:

```text
remembered trials:                   13
remembered trials correctly matched: 13 / 13
not_remembered trials:                5
conservative treatment:               all 5 = misses
conservative total:                   13 / 18
frozen threshold:                     13 / 18
```

All 13 informative remembered trials were correctly attributed using concrete lived details rather than Thread IDs, seed metadata, EventStructure IDs, provider metadata, or assignment digests.

Therefore E2's central downstream-fertility question is supported in this development sample:

```text
particular lived routes
        ↓
genome-blind selective memory
        ↓
history/genome-blind durable meaning
        ↓
source life remains distinguishable
```

The conservative 13/18 statement is the canonical interpretation. The raw 16/18 number must not be presented as fully grounded evidence.

## What E2 established

E2 supports the following development conclusions:

1. **Offer breadth is insufficient.** A rich pool can still yield interchangeable lives when event choice and grounding convenience are coupled.
2. **Selection/realization coupling was a real mechanism of collapse.** Separating opportunity occurrence from scene realization materially expanded cast/route realization.
3. **Deterministic single-winner selection is not the answer.** It can create a higher-level opportunity-template monoculture.
4. **Seeded contingency over a mechanically eligible opportunity surface is the strongest mechanism observed so far.** It preserved factual continuity without feeding prior-life diversity goals back into selection.
5. **The resulting particularity was autobiographically fertile in the development sample.** All 13 informative remembered N1 trials remained source-identifiable; pessimistically counting every no-memory case as a failure still met the frozen 13/18 threshold.
6. **The plausibility-model layer was non-discriminating on the two diagnostic worlds.** It should not be treated as part of the supported mechanism without separate evidence.

## What E2 did not establish

E2 does **not** establish:

- that more events, places, people, intellectual encounters, novelty, drama, or consequence automatically make a life richer;
- that every life should maximize breadth;
- that every experience should become a memory;
- that every memory should acquire durable meaning;
- that the exact A2b development runner is the final production implementation;
- that two diagnostic worlds constitute final-cohort evidence;
- that genome causes later identity or behavior;
- that #39 earns Whole-Person causal standing;
- an admission gate based on richness scores or quotas.

Rich Life remains about **non-interchangeable lived experience with downstream potential**, not a checklist.

## Remaining caveats carried forward

1. **Development-world scope.** D1/D2 and all E2 lives are burned; they cannot be reused for final G/H evidence.
2. **Locality concentration.** Some A2b lives remained place-concentrated; D2 seed-03 had only two places and a 0.70 top-place share. This is characterization, not a failed quota.
3. **Scene survivorship pressure.** A2b required 8 candidate attempts for 6 completed lives, with 2 whole-candidate rejections. The frozen opportunity schedule prevented route substitution, but easier-to-encode scene realizations may still survive disproportionately.
4. **N1 no-memory scoring defect.** The forced 2AFC on `not_remembered` cases was methodologically weak; those trials contribute no positive credit in the conservative result.
5. **N1 execution amendments.** The bounded Pass-B form profile and deterministic no-memory residue canonicalization are fully recorded and must remain visible to hostile review.
6. **Meaning-rate characterization.** All 13 remembered N1 cases produced durable meaning; that observed rate is not a normative requirement or calibrated natural frequency.

## Mechanism carried forward

The development mechanism to carry into the next fresh-cohort design is conceptually:

```text
public WorldSpec + age/time window
        ↓
mechanically eligible ordinary-life opportunity surface
        ↓
Thread-blind / history-stateless world contingency
        ↓
fixed opportunity
        ↓
factual continuity-aware realization
        ↓
canonical Thread history
        ↓
selective memory and possible meaning
```

No anti-repeat objective, diversity quota, richness score, desired-personality target, cumulative-life quality feedback, or future-outcome optimization belongs in the opportunity choice.

Whether the eventual production implementation needs an LLM plausibility layer remains open; D1/D2 supplied no evidence for it.
