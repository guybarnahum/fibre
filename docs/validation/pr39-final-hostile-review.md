## What #39 genuinely established

### 1. A generated prior life can become canonical life without changing its admitted facts

The final candidates are not profile JSON promoted by convention. Birth materializes admitted history into canonical Thread events, autobiographical memory into the memory authority, people/lineage and places into situated-life authorities, symbolic inheritance into genome/lineage authority, visual-companion obligations into identity state, and FIN registration into the Civil Registry.

The independent hydration proof closes the central #39 publication risk: these authorities reconstruct the same admitted lives after publication.

### 2. Birth is atomic at the Thread boundary

`GenesisStore.publishBirth()` performs the live Thread transition inside one database transaction. Its publication trigger inserts the FIN/civil registration from the admitted manifest inside that transaction. Existing tests prove failures after seed, situated continuity, lineage or memory work roll back rather than leaving a partially born Thread, and the place-authority test proves a narration/place contradiction commits no Thread, manifest, envelope or civil registration.

The five-Thread closure runner itself is **resumable, not one five-Thread transaction**. Atomicity is per Thread birth. The observed Sapporo-first interruption demonstrates the distinction: Sapporo remained a valid completed birth while Kochi had not been born. Recovery then rehydrated Sapporo exactly and continued the remaining births.

Do not describe the whole five-Thread batch as one atomic transaction.

### 3. Civil identity is not a second Thread identity authority

Each born Thread has one unique FIN bound to its canonical machine `threadId`, seed birth event, World and registration time. Hydration resolves the registry record and requires exact equality with the registration embedded in the Genesis manifest.

The FIN is a permanent human-facing civil identifier; `threadId` remains the canonical machine identity/reference anchor. The Identity Card remains a derived credential rather than a third identity authority.

### 4. Recovery is execution evidence, not life evidence

The final cohort survived two different operational recovery seams:

- durable provider-call replay during generation/diagnostics;
- the birth-publication correction discovered when Kochi's second meaning revision attempted to erase an earlier supporting-evidence reference.

The birth correction changed only canonical evidence-lineage materialization: later meaning revisions now carry the cumulative set of previously cited supporting evidence. It did not change the frozen candidate, remembered content, remembered meaning, reinterpretation output, World, genome or candidate digest. Sapporo's already committed FIN and birth were preserved.

Mechanical recovery and replay prove execution/restart properties only. They do not support claims about identity, memory quality, meaning, character or personhood.

## Scientific/generator findings that remain non-blocking debt

### A. D1 is distinguishability, not non-interchangeability

The frozen D1 arithmetic is valid for the actual stateless five-way design (`p=0.2` per target; five successes under that scorer give `0.2^5 = 0.00032`). But raw and explicit-entity-normalized 5/5 attribution demonstrates that the five texts are distinguishable, not that Fibre has established causal historical particularity.

Named-entity normalization was not culture-neutral. Environmental and cultural fingerprints remained available to the rater. The permanent #39 record must keep the narrower interpretation.

### B. The cohort has real differentiation and real template reuse

The five routes differ materially in deterministic historical skeleton and remembered content. The pre-birth hostile inspection found Pass-B remembered content strongly differentiated and identified specific residues unique to individual Threads.

But the same inspection also found generator regularities that weaken any stronger claim:

- Sapporo and Wellington share `ges_v2_mundane_errand_independence` at the same window, both selected it as memory 2, and produced near-paraphrase errand memories;
- the EventStructure pool contains near-duplicate `mentor_optional_path` / `optional_path_from_mentor` and `choose_against_peer_group` / `choose_against_peer_preference` pairs;
- the optional-path pair produced closely similar meanings in Sapporo, Kochi and Wellington;
- teacher/library routes recur across all five Threads;
- Pass-C meaning text carries a strong common generator register that is much weaker in Pass-B remembered content.

These are generator-characterization findings. They do not invalidate the fixed cohort, but they must not disappear from the milestone record or be rewritten as evidence of five behaviorally non-interchangeable people.

### C. The predeclared Sapporo/Kochi convergence is weak

Sapporo and Kochi did show a specific first-memory selection convergence on an adult-takeover/passive-compliance affordance despite highly different historical skeletons. But that is not the predeclared practical-reliability theme, and the strongest apparent later "collect details / keep the option open" convergence is reproduced by Wellington and entangled with the near-duplicate EventStructure pool.

The honest record is **weak/off-hypothesis convergence**, not confirmation of the predeclared convergent-pair theme.

### D. D4 established opportunity-level saturation, not that every historical episode was archived

All `30/30` scheduled Pass-B opportunities returned `remembered`, and all 30 current memories have durable meaning. That proves the scheduled memory/meaning funnel failed to exercise its legal decline/no-meaning paths in this cohort.

It does **not** mean every historical episode was remembered by every Thread. Unique historical episodes cited by memory were:

```text

Kochi        12 / 14
Malmö         6 / 14
Valparaíso   14 / 14
Wellington    6 / 14
```

Sapporo, Malmö and Wellington therefore retained substantial episode-level historical excess even though every scheduled memory call produced a memory. Kochi sits between those cases; Valparaíso is the genuinely saturated/over-authored extreme.

The permanent language should therefore say **scheduled-memory-call saturation / insufficient non-selection**, not "Genesis archives everything".

A second restraint concern remains: 10 reinterpretations ran and 9 revised while only 1 remained unchanged. This is follow-up evidence about meaning/reinterpretation restraint, not a statistically calibrated defect rate.

### E. D2 is underpowered; positive reframing is post-hoc

The six-pair-per-Thread Spearman diagnostic does not support strong cohort inference. The observed approximately `+0.50` mean valence uplift, with 16/30 meanings more positive, 3/30 more negative and 11/30 tied, is a descriptive post-hoc signal only.

It motivates the accepted pre-#40 meaning-fidelity work; it is not promoted into a confirmatory #39 result.

### F. D3 is inconclusive

The memory-only genome exposure boundary itself remained controlled: the `life_only_unexposed` cells occur before any genome-exposed remembered memory and genome material is visible only in the declared Pass-B treatment cells.

But the D3 effect instrument cannot resolve genome propagation because treatment cell is perfectly entangled with call ordinal/history horizon, cells are small, calibration is easier than the actual task, and the intended `not_remembered` contrast is empty.

Therefore #39 neither establishes nor rules out genome propagation. The clean cognition-exposure boundary must not be confused with a clean causal estimator.

### G. D5 did not independently measure self-account overreach

The intended construct remains useful, but `65/70` episode accommodation judgments were predicted by memory citation. The executed D5 therefore mostly re-derived D4 coverage.

Valparaíso's 14/14 citation breadth and zero unaccommodated episodes are an extreme saturation fixture, not evidence that its self-account is safely non-totalizing.

The accepted pre-#40 bridge owns the prospective sealed-history redesign with compiler-enforced exclusion and positive/negative/counterfactual controls.

### H. Temperature-zero repeats are robustness checks

The three stateless temperature-zero calls with option-order permutation test order sensitivity/response stability. They are not independent scientific replicates. The 180 committed evaluator judgments must not be described as 180 independent observations.
