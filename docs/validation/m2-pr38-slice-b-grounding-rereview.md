## Re-review the three prior blockers

1. **Grounded lived facts.** Relation/place writes must resolve persisted Fibre evidence before they become part of the Thread's life. Missing evidence cannot create a parent, genome-source parent, residence, or other situated fact. Retraction/correction must preserve history while removing false claims from the current life view.

2. **Continuity of lived history.** Relation/place revisions are predecessor-chained and independently head-bound. The relevant property is that a Thread's earlier lived past cannot silently become a different past while later identity still treats it as the same historical basis.

3. **Grounded situated meaning.** Lineage, culture, language, geography, and place meaning are `context_only` in #38. Durable admission requires the corresponding persisted relation/place/lived-event witness. Place, ancestry, language, appearance, and voice may describe context; they may not directly mint temperament, values, competence, willingness, or fluency.
