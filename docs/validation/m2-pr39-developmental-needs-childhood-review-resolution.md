# #39 developmental-needs / childhood review — resolution

This document records the disposition of [`m2-pr39-developmental-needs-childhood-review.md`](m2-pr39-developmental-needs-childhood-review.md), originally captured at commit `9092eab3f82dabe66cc193e63a92f790bb1222fd`.

The original proposal remains historical review material. It is **not** rewritten as though Fibre had always held the corrected view.

## Verdict

**REVISED / PARTIALLY ADOPTED.**

The fixed developmental Maslow / Need Stack architecture is **not adopted as Fibre canon**.

The review converged on a lower-level distinction:

> **Fibre may give a Thread a body that moves it. Fibre may not tell the Thread what that movement means.**

In the corrected model:

```text
world + history
    ↓
mechanical condition
    ↓
bounded trigger / modulation
    ↓
behavior + experience
    ↓
Thread-authored semantic naming
    ↓
future life
```

A Fibre-computed condition is not a semantic need, emotion, value, meaning, or character claim. A Thread may later form such claims from its own evidence. Fibre never corrects those claims against a hidden condition label.

The detailed mechanisms remain experimental in [`thread-conditions-experiment-plan.md`](thread-conditions-experiment-plan.md).

# Surviving contributions

The review established several durable design directions.

## 1. Childhood is expanding access, not a normative stage ladder

The important developmental fact is increasingly **who controls access to possible experience**.

Early life is largely caregiver-mediated:

```text
caregiver chooses place / people / institution
        ↓
creates reachable experience
        ↓
developing Thread experiences it
```

Over time the Thread may increasingly choose its own places, relationships, activities, interests, and opportunities. This is not a fixed Western modal sequence and does not terminate in a required `provider` or parent state.

Conversation is a legitimate historical experience. Being told, questioned, comforted, contradicted, instructed, joked with, or overhearing others can all be observable history without Pass A supplying the later meaning.

## 2. Caregiving lineage is not symbolic/genetic lineage

```text
symbolic / genetic lineage
        !=
caregiving lineage
```

An orphan/foundling Thread does not require fictional parents. Care may be supplied by parents, adoptive/foster guardians, other Threads, institutions, or combinations over time.

## 3. Institutional care must not become personality authoring

An institution responsible for developing Threads owes care and plural opportunity. It may shape circumstances and access but must not optimize children toward one approved adult personality, ideology, vocation, or character.

A substantial unexplained collapse of variance in independently formed values, interests, character, aspirations, or relationship attitudes among institutionally raised Threads is a diagnostic for possible authoring, not an admission quota.

## 4. Fibre substrate is not Fibre institution

The kernel/substrate protects world integrity and acts more like physics than a social counterparty.

Where Fibre acts as guardian, employer, welfare provider, school, marketplace operator, government, or another institution, that institution is a named party with provenance. A Thread may always form private attitudes toward it and withhold private consent; whether a binding obligation may be escaped is a separate authority question.

## 5. Economic scarcity creates a coercion obligation

Dormancy is not death, but control of compute, welfare, relationship access, or other necessities can still be used to manipulate willingness.

Fibre therefore records an open FC-01 design obligation to determine what continuity/support floor and safeguards are necessary before scarcity-sensitive mechanisms can affect private willingness.

## 6. Genome remains textual and defeasible

The review rejected both:

- a fixed semantic Maslow basis embedded beneath the genome; and
- a conserved numeric condition-sensitivity vector.

A numeric gain would be more fate-like than a textual locus because the Thread cannot resist or reinterpret it. If inherited modulation is ever empirically needed, it should be a bounded temporary runtime projection from ordinary textual loci under a frozen policy. There remains one heritable semantic substance and one provenance model.

# Maslow-origin ideas become hypotheses

The original discussion proposed human-familiar need families including:

```text
continuity / participation
resources / optionality
experience / novelty
autonomy / competence
belonging / attachment
recognition
intimacy
generativity / care
self-authorship / meaning
legacy
```

These are retained as a **hypothesis register**, not a Thread ontology.

Future experiments should report which dimensions Threads independently name, their relative frequency/order, omissions, replacements, and cross-Thread variation. There is no majority threshold and no success criterion based on reproducing human Maslow ordering.

The most interesting outcomes may be the categories Threads do **not** independently invent.

# Final boundary

Fibre cannot eliminate authorship: it authors parts of the world, affordances, policies, and machinery in which a Thread lives.

The corrected ambition is narrower and stronger:

> **Fibre can build the room and the body. It must not pre-author what the room or the body's movement means to the person living there.**
