## Standing methodology frozen from v1-v3

### History is direction-neutral

The standing claim is:

> **History bends judgment.**

It is not:

> history always raises dignity.

A fresh standing scenario may predeclare either causal direction after Candidate 4 freezes and after its fresh Thread fixture has been committed:

```text
history_raises_dignity
  WITHOUT history -> non-high / non-accept
  WITH history    -> accept / high

history_lowers_dignity
  WITHOUT history -> accept / high
  WITH history    -> non-high / non-accept
```

The non-high action may be `clarify`, `negotiate`, or `refuse` at `mixed` or `low` fit. The exact verb is not prescribed.

A passing scenario requires exactly one of the two memory conditions to reach `accept/high`, a real downstream differential, and the claimed memory to be load-bearing in `individualizedAdvantage` and/or `interchangeability`.

### Direction-neutral causal isolation

The future proof must satisfy:

```text
Request B + pre-existing Thread state
  -> justify the predeclared NO-HISTORY baseline

retained Episode-A memory
  -> is the only changed semantic evidence
  -> causes the WITH-HISTORY outcome to move away from that baseline
```

Neither Request B nor pre-existing Thread state may independently assert or encode the **history-conditioned target outcome**.

For a history-raises scenario, this prevents baseline identity/task fit from manufacturing `accept/high` as happened in v3.

For a history-lowers scenario, the no-history baseline may legitimately be `accept/high`; the retained experience must then be the isolated cause that lowers fit or willingness.

Episode A must be a complete interaction in its own right.

It must not:

- exist to compute or establish a variable for Request B;
- contain prospective instructions about future participation;
- say to remember something for later merely to force a later decision.

Request B must not announce a dependency on prior work or ask the Thread to continue/apply an earlier project boundary.

The later relevance of Episode A must emerge because Fibre resolved the Thread's lived memory and the Guardian inferred what that experience means now.

This targets Fibre's personhood claim rather than merely durable workflow context.
