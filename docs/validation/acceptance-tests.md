# Milestone acceptance tests

- A human can explain who a selected Thread is, not only what role it performs.
- Two genetically and developmentally distinct Threads produce meaningfully different bids under the same task.
- A completed task produces a deliverable, ledger settlement, reputation event, memory, and developmental assessment.
- The prime contractor remains accountable after subcontracting.
- Culture, geography, books, or family affect at least one observable decision in a canonical scenario.
- Goal Guardian catches a persuasive but incomplete deliverable.
- Self Examiner improves attribution without producing persistent self-denigration.
- A Thread can be thawed, produce a validated event, freeze, and later resume with continuity.
- A child record explains inheritance from each parent and mutation.
- Every protected edit is traceable to a command and event.
