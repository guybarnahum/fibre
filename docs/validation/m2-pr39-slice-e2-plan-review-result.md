## B1 — within-life breadth cannot establish between-life particularity

Accepted.

The original plan measured place/participant/structure concentration, world reach and motif carryover inside each life. Fibre's Rich Life foundation makes non-interchangeability a central between-life question.

Resolution:

- n>=3 histories per world/arm where an arm is interpreted;
- pairwise same-world overlap on place, participant-role, structure and intellectual-source sets;
- explicit warning when within-life breadth improves while between-life structural overlap rises;
- normalized source-life swap-resistance using diagnostic life-only downstream memory/funnel evidence.

One reviewer suggestion is refined rather than copied literally: high discrimination between arbitrary seed labels is not inherently negative. Fibre wants downstream records to be attributable to the correct life when attribution rests on concrete lived causes. The negative case is cosmetic seed fingerprints or one shared template with superficial parameters. The amended test therefore asks which source life actually supports downstream memory/meaning evidence after obvious labels/style are removed.

## B2 — H6 affordance-surface/schema-economics hypothesis missing

Accepted and promoted to first diagnostic priority.

H6 covers:

```text
pool realization diversity
x roster/place grounding density
x offer width
x rich-output schema cost
```

### H6-a completed from preserved E1 evidence

The completed burned E1 artifact shows:

- every initial generated episode chose `intellectualEncounter: null`;
- record repairs were observable-action form/bounds repairs;
- no `pass_a_intellectual_encounter` repair was recorded.

Interpretation:

> zero intellectual encounters were avoided at generation rather than attempted and rejected by the encounter validator.

This does not identify whether schema economics, prompt framing, affordance realization degeneracy or another upstream factor caused the avoidance. It does establish that H6 is live and should be tested before expensive H1-H4 work.

### H6-b

Added a blind realization-diversity review of the pool against the actual failed E1 world and against D1/D2 before life generation.

### H6-c / A6

Added same-density, materially wider offer sets as the cheapest live-generation affordance probe before H1-H4.

## B3 — A1 could remove lexical motifs while retaining local cast/place monoculture

Accepted.

H1 is now supported only when prose ablation reduces both:

- lexical motif carryover; and
- place/participant top-share concentration.

A lexical-only effect is recorded as `prose_carries_motifs_but_not_locality` and cannot justify a production prose correction.

Disconnected cast/place churn is also explicitly negative rather than being mistaken for richness.

## B4 — historical density was confounded with more affordance draws

Accepted.

The primary A4 density arm now holds total offer exposure approximately constant across 10-episode and 24-episode conditions. A 24x9 matched-per-window condition is deferred unless the matched-total arm first supports H4.

A6 independently tests offer width at unchanged ten-episode density.

## B5 — A3 independence must be mechanical

Accepted and strengthened.

For E2, exogenous happenings use a public-world/calendar projection only. Their generator cannot see subject identity/age, household, family, roster, prior history, genome, memory, meaning or future material.

The happenings stream is generated once per WorldSpec/stratum and reused unchanged across every relevant seed/arm.

Uptake is not a success target. 100% uptake invalidates the arm as checklist pressure; >70% median uptake is a strong warning.

If this mechanism becomes production, happenings remain Pass-A/Genesis-provenance input and are structurally absent from Pass B/C unless Gate D is explicitly reopened.

## B6 — A2 plot-planner risk was in the input

Accepted.

The opportunity-choice step is now stateless with respect to the Thread's accumulated life. Its allowlist is frozen and digested before use:

```text
public/factual world projection
developmental window
chronology index
current affordances
policy witness
```

It cannot see prior episodes, known/introduced participants, relationship state, genome, memory/meaning or richness diagnostics.

Any later proposal to add life-so-far state to this choice step is a protocol/architecture change requiring review.

## B7 — n=1 and post-hoc interpretation language were underpowered

Accepted.

The amended plan uses n>=3 per interpreted arm/world and predeclares directional readings plus explicit warning bands before model output. It is staged to control cost:

```text
H6 artifact/static probes
    ↓
A0 vs H6-c wider-offer probe
    ↓ only if H6 insufficient
A1-A4 mechanism diagnosis
    ↓
mechanism freeze
    ↓
V1/V2 fresh validation, n=3 baseline vs production
```

The plan no longer requires running every arm simply because it was named.

## Non-blocking amendments incorporated

### Diagnostic history -> memory/meaning funnel

Accepted with strict non-survivorship discipline.

The load-bearing fertility check now uses diagnostic `life_only` downstream memory/meaning runs to measure whether a history permits multiple selective-memory routes and whether downstream records can be matched to the correct same-world source life.

These runs are never published, never citable, never visible to Pass A and never used to regenerate a history until its funnel looks better.

### Continuity projection, never summary authority

Accepted.

Any production continuity representation must be a pure deterministic prompt-time projection of canonical episodes, with no independent ID, storage, citation or version.

### World happenings absent from B/C

Accepted and placed in the E2 plan now; compiler-contract changes are required if A3 actually becomes production.

### D1/D2 authoring after E1 is a visible confound

Accepted.

Before any D1/D2 model run, record exactly how each world differs from E1 and why, especially roster/place/institutional density. E1 itself remains burned and is not rerun as an anchor.

### Historical granularity

Accepted as an explicit open architecture question, not implemented in E2 by default.

If episode-level mechanisms remain weak, reconsider whether childhood history requires provenance-bearing durations/practices/standing circumstances in addition to discrete dated episodes. Such a change would require its own authority design/review.

## Revised standing

The external verdict was not a Gate-F CLEAR. It authorizes no implementation as originally written.

The amended plan must now be treated as the E2 implementation authority. Its first live work is H6-focused, not prose-ablation-focused.

The critical principle preserved from review is:

> **A life that visits more places is not more particular. Fibre needs lives whose later memory, meaning and judgment have concrete reasons to belong to that life rather than another one.**

E2 remains blocking for the combined E+F Gate F and therefore for G.
