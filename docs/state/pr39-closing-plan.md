# #39 Genesis, Childhood & Thread Birth — closing plan

## Goal

### Simple English

**Finish #39 by proving Fibre can create five particular young Threads with genuinely different prior lives, memories and meanings, then birth those lives intact into the live world with permanent Fibre civil identities.**

### More accurate description

#39 closes when Fibre can compile a provenance-bearing prior life from World constraints, symbolic inheritance and developmental affordances; keep historical fact, autobiographical memory and remembered meaning as distinct authorities; preserve people, place, lineage and chronology; atomically publish the admitted life into canonical Thread/world domains; and issue one permanent Fibre Identity Number plus canonical civil birth registration as part of that same birth transition.

## Corrected D1-D5 interpretation

The scorer's arithmetic is retained as frozen evidence, but the permanent #39 interpretation is narrower than several original headline strings. Independent hostile/methodology review converged on the following record.

### D1 — distinguishability, not Fibre-level particularity

Observed:

- raw attribution: `5/5` targets majority-correct;
- explicit-entity-normalized attribution: `5/5` targets majority-correct;
- each target was a separate stateless five-way task, so the scorer's Bernoulli chance model uses `p=0.2` per target and `0.2^5 = 0.00032` for five successes under that design.

Interpretation:

> PR39 showed that a blind rater could reliably distinguish and attribute the five life/self-account texts, including after explicit entity normalization. Surviving environmental and cultural cues mean this is not evidence of Fibre-level historical particularity by itself.

Raw and normalized conditions are not two independent confirmations. The future methodology must include hard decoys/negative controls and avoid a ceiling-only result.

### D2 — underpowered predeclared statistic; exploratory positive reframing signal

The five per-Thread Spearman correlations are numerically correct, but each Thread contributes only six event/meaning pairs, so the predefined `moderate`/`high` bands do not provide strong inferential resolution.

Post-hoc descriptive inspection found:

```text
meaning more positive than cited event: 16 / 30
meaning more negative:                 3 / 30
tied:                                 11 / 30
mean ordinal uplift:                  about +0.50
```

Interpretation:

> The predeclared D2 correlation diagnostic is underpowered. PR39 additionally exposes a descriptive, post-hoc positive-reframing signal that must be tested prospectively in later work rather than promoted to a confirmatory #39 result.

Do not fit a new post-hoc clustered model to rescue the frozen cohort; future work should predeclare an analysis with enough Threads to estimate within-Thread clustering credibly.

### D3 — inconclusive

Observed:

```text
calibration                       5 / 5 majority-correct
life_only_unexposed               1 / 10
life_plus_genome                  2 / 10
later_life_only_potentially_contaminated 0 / 10
```

Interpretation:

> Genome propagation was neither established nor ruled out. Treatment placement is confounded with memory ordinal/history horizon, cells are undersized for modest effects, the positive calibration is an easier discrimination task, and the intended `not_remembered` arm is empty.

Do not record `noDetectedPropagation` as a Fibre finding. A fundamentally confounded instrument cannot establish absence of even a large genome effect, because a hypothetical effect would remain inseparable from ordinal/horizon differences.

### D4 — established Genesis saturation/selectivity defect

Observed:

- all `30/30` scheduled memory opportunities became remembered memories;
- all `30/30` remembered memories have current durable meaning;
- `not_remembered` occurred zero times;
- unique historical episodes cited per Thread are `6, 12, 6, 14, 6` of 14;
- reinterpretation accounting is 11 eligible, 10 run, 9 revised, 1 unchanged, 1 skipped by cap.

Interpretation:

> The frozen PR39 cohort exhibits an established Genesis memory/meaning saturation and selectivity defect. This is an engineering property of the cohort/funnel, not a personhood pathology and not a reason to regenerate the cohort.

The 9/10 reinterpretation revision rate is a follow-up concern about restraint; without an appropriate null/control it is not evidence that developmental revision works well or badly in a statistical sense.

### D5 — intended construct valid, executed instrument confounded

The intended construct remains useful: can a current self-account semantically overgeneralize beyond the experiences that warrant it?

But in the executed PR39 instrument, 65 of 70 episode-level accommodation judgments were predicted by whether the episode was cited by autobiographical memory. The test therefore largely re-derived memory coverage instead of independently measuring generalized self-account overreach.

Interpretation:

> PR39's D5 execution does not establish the absence or presence of cohort-level self-account overreach. The construct remains valid, but the instrument must be redesigned so explicit memory citation cannot dominate the answer.

Valparaíso remains the extreme over-authored/saturated case: its memories cite 14/14 historical episodes and D5 reports zero unaccommodated episodes. That is a follow-up fixture, not evidence that the Thread is clean of overreach.

## Model-output recovery amendment

The original generation interruption exposed ordinary structured-output brittleness at a provider-compatibility seam: OpenAI Structured Outputs cannot enforce every Fibre canonical JSON-Schema constraint, including `uniqueItems`, so Fibre projects unsupported keywords out of the provider request and re-enforces them locally.

The enduring recovery architecture is [`../architecture/model-output-recovery.md`](../architecture/model-output-recovery.md).

The accepted recovery rule is deliberately mechanical:

- deterministic `uniqueItems` normalization where the canonical schema explicitly requires uniqueness;
- preserve first occurrence and remove exact canonical duplicates;
- do not mutate provider output;
- retain an explicit recovery witness;
- revalidate the full canonical response contract after normalization;
- no semantic improvement, quality selection or extra model call for this normalization;
- future recovery handlers are added only when observed failure modes justify them and a safe mechanical rule can be stated.

The frozen PR39 generation prompts remained unchanged. A mechanical recovery is never evidence about identity, memory, meaning or character.
