# Current priorities

1. Preserve the fully closed deterministic M1 Persistent Thread Round Trip as historical executable evidence, while treating the canonical pre-M2 service merged in PR #31 as the live appraisal/runtime baseline. Historical M1 evidence must not be silently inherited as proof of semantic individuality or Development on the canonical service.
2. Execute the accepted [`Pre-M2 bridge plan`](../validation/pre-m2-bridge-plan.md) and refined [`M2 PR plan`](../validation/m2-pr-plan.md) in order. #33 and #34 are **EARNED / SEALED**. **#35 Structured Obligation v1 is active**, followed by #36 M2 Identity & Embodiment Contract, #37 Thread Passport & Identity Provenance v1, #38 Lineage/Geography/Embodiment v1, #39 Identity Projection & Causal Consumption, and #40 M2 Standing Gate / closure. Post-M2 sequence begins with #41 Self-authored Development, #42 Reciprocal Relationships, and #43 Economic Consequence / M3 foundation.
3. Preserve #33 Semantic Guardian standing evidence and methodology without tuning against sealed v1-v4 results. Continue to defend against assistant-mode collapse, unsupported factors, provider/protocol failure being mistaken for a Thread decision, persistent self-conditioning, caller-authored private state selection, and semantic-state absence being treated as neutral evidence.
4. Preserve **Semantic State v0** as defined by [`emotions-and-needs.md`](../concepts/emotions-and-needs.md): natural-language-first state; closed domains with registered extensible dimensions; evidence for every persisted change; `asOf`, supersession, provenance, staleness, restricted visibility, descriptive-not-instructional validation, and Fibre-owned bounded state selection. Requester-specific relationship attitudes remain **Semantic Relationship State v0**.
5. Preserve #34's earned but deliberately limited Development `0 -> 1` claim: a Fibre-owned durable record formed through an earlier canonical episode survived restart and causally changed a later appraisal under exact one-memory withholding. Do **not** silently reinterpret this as rich self-authored learning. The current memory is deterministic episode/request-derived description; Thread-authored reflection, experience-derived self-model/state change, adverse/low-dignity experience memory, and repeated cross-episode learning remain explicit extension targets.
6. For #35, follow [`structured-obligations-v1.md`](../architecture/structured-obligations-v1.md). Structured obligations use stable IDs, append-only revisions, issuer/parties, scope/terms, effective/expiry state, recurrence, satisfaction criteria, provenance, separate standing/terms visibility, and request-bound applicability evidence. **Fibre determines applicability; caller nomination is attention, never authority. Compulsion never rewrites consent.**
7. Keep #35's transition explicitly sliced: **A)** domain + additive schema + legacy-spend tombstones; **B)** ObligationStore/current-revision integrity; **C)** persisted Fibre applicability; **D)** runtime authorization cutover; **E)** freeze/discharge cutover; **F)** inspector + restart/replay/adversarial closure. Slice A is non-authoritative: legacy `obligationReferences` remains historical compatibility until D. Do not claim #35 closed merely because the new tables exist.
8. Never auto-promote `currentState.unresolvedIntentions` into active obligations. The legacy field includes personal unfinished intentions and cannot establish commitment authority. Preserve already-consumed exact legacy references as deterministic tombstones so pre-migration spent authority cannot resurrect. Legitimate active obligations require explicit authoritative classification.
9. Define the M2 Identity and Embodiment contract in #36 only after #35 closes the authority gap. The contract covers durable Thread passport, portrait and voice provenance, geography timeline, family/lineage representation, privacy boundaries, mutation/version rules, self-authorship, and falsifiable behavioral acceptance scenarios. Different compiled identity context alone is not M2 closure.
10. Treat durable, targeted, evidence-backed relationship attitudes introduced through Semantic State v0 as **Semantic Relationship State v0**, the first layer of a relationship aggregate. Do not award broader social/relationship credit until richer reciprocal relationship mechanisms change later appraisal or future possibility. Keep the broader relationship service deferred: reciprocal/shared relationship structures, commitments/expectations, repair processes, relationship-specific permissions, family/social roles, and richer relational mechanisms remain later work.
11. Keep the general worker/tool/model gateway **deferred** during the pre-M2 bridge. A narrowly scoped Guardian model adapter is permitted for appraisal only; Actor remains deterministic and tool/network incapable, and Goal Guardian remains an audit layer rather than a production sandbox.
12. Keep the API-backed Thread Editor non-authoritative. Every editor API request requires its per-run credential; the editor may inspect public/private lifecycle and expression records and request previews, but may not seed, accept commands, issue participation authority, write disclosure or response records, acquire runtime, run workers, freeze, abandon, repair, or mutate obligations.
13. Keep command acceptance and projection repair behind administrative authority. Command preview remains public and non-mutating; preview identity is derivable from its public receipt, so the admin token—not redaction—is the authority boundary.
14. Continue targeted adversarial hardening, especially mutation baselines, crash/concurrency cases, cross-connection races, source-integrity reporting, model-evaluation holdout discipline, semantic-state injection/staleness/selection discipline, cross-Thread private-state leakage, and every new obligation authority path introduced by #35.
15. Design real external delivery and performed-action records as separate future capabilities. Do not infer delivery or action from an M1 audience-response record, Actor output, authorization, or freeze.
16. Execute the autonomous web-product-studio task-marketplace vertical slice only after identity, embodiment, relationships, and the worker/tool gateway are reliable.
17. Preserve the broader Fibre ambition—social, familial, cultural, institutional, developmental, and economic life—while requiring each new layer to pass persistence, provenance, authorization, replay, adversarial-evidence, causal-differentiation, and human-inspection standards.
