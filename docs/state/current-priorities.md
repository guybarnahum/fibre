Historical M1 evidence must not be silently inherited as proof of semantic individuality or Development on the canonical service.
2. Post-M2 sequence begins with #41 Self-authored Development, #42 Reciprocal Relationships, and #43 Economic Consequence / M3 foundation.
3. Preserve #33 Semantic Guardian standing evidence and methodology without tuning against sealed v1-v4 results. Continue to defend against assistant-mode collapse, unsupported factors, provider/protocol failure being mistaken for a Thread decision, persistent self-conditioning, caller-authored private state selection, and semantic-state absence being treated as neutral evidence.
4. Preserve **Semantic State v0** as defined by [`emotions-and-needs.md`](../concepts/emotions-and-needs.md): natural-language-first state; closed domains with registered extensible dimensions; evidence for every persisted change; `asOf`, supersession, provenance, staleness, restricted visibility, descriptive-not-instructional validation, and Fibre-owned bounded state selection. Requester-specific relationship attitudes remain **Semantic Relationship State v0**.
5. Preserve #34's earned but deliberately limited Development `0 -> 1` claim: a Fibre-owned durable record formed through an earlier canonical episode survived restart and causally changed a later appraisal under exact one-memory withholding. Do **not** silently reinterpret this as rich self-authored learning. The current memory is deterministic episode/request-derived description; Thread-authored reflection, experience-derived self-model/state change, adverse/low-dignity experience memory, and repeated cross-episode learning remain explicit extension targets.
6. Preserve #35's Structured Obligation authority invariants from [`structured-obligations-v1.md`](../architecture/structured-obligations-v1.md): **caller nomination is attention, never authority; Fibre determines applicability; compulsion never rewrites consent; completed one-shot compelled participation must leave an atomic durable discharge consequence.** Historical applicability is evidence, not a perpetual capability.
7. Treat #35 A-F as one reviewed integrity chain: **A)** domain/schema/migration + legacy-spend tombstones; **B)** ObligationStore/current-revision integrity; **C)** persisted Fibre applicability; **D)** runtime authorization cutover; **E)** atomic freeze/discharge cutover; **F)** read-only private/admin inspection with restart/replay/privacy/adversarial closure. Do not expand #35 during review into semantic natural-language applicability, recurrence semantics, editor mutation, or personhood-score work.
8. Never auto-promote `currentState.unresolvedIntentions` into active obligations. The legacy field includes personal unfinished intentions and cannot establish commitment authority. Preserve already-consumed exact legacy references as deterministic tombstones so pre-migration spent authority cannot resurrect. Legitimate active obligations require explicit authoritative classification.
9. Preserve F's inspection boundary: live obligation inspection is private-token, loopback-only, GET-only and `no-store`; the verifier opens the database read-only with SQLite `query_only`; it performs no repair; public Thread/event/health routes expose no private obligation evidence. The same cross-chain verifier is available administratively through `npm run inspect:obligations`.
10. Define the M2 Identity and Embodiment contract in #36 only after #35 review/merge. The contract covers durable Thread passport, portrait and voice provenance, geography timeline, family/lineage representation, privacy boundaries, mutation/version rules, self-authorship, and falsifiable behavioral acceptance scenarios. Different compiled identity context alone is not M2 closure.
11. Treat durable, targeted, evidence-backed relationship attitudes introduced through Semantic State v0 as **Semantic Relationship State v0**, the first layer of a relationship aggregate. Do not award broader social/relationship credit until richer reciprocal relationship mechanisms change later appraisal or future possibility. Keep the broader relationship service deferred: reciprocal/shared relationship structures, commitments/expectations, repair processes, relationship-specific permissions, family/social roles, and richer relational mechanisms remain later work.
12. Keep the general worker/tool/model gateway **deferred** during the pre-M2 bridge. A narrowly scoped Guardian model adapter is permitted for appraisal only; Actor remains deterministic and tool/network incapable, and Goal Guardian remains an audit layer rather than a production sandbox.
13. Keep the API-backed Thread Editor non-authoritative. Every editor API request requires its per-run credential; the editor may inspect its existing public/private lifecycle and expression records and request previews, but may not seed, accept commands, issue participation authority, write disclosure or response records, acquire runtime, run workers, freeze, abandon, repair, or mutate obligations. #35 F adds kernel/private and offline administrative obligation inspection without granting new editor authority.
14. Keep command acceptance and projection repair behind administrative authority. Command preview remains public and non-mutating; preview identity is derivable from its public receipt, so the admin token—not redaction—is the authority boundary.
15. Continue targeted adversarial hardening, especially mutation baselines, crash/concurrency cases, cross-connection races, source-integrity reporting, model-evaluation holdout discipline, semantic-state injection/staleness/selection discipline, cross-Thread private-state leakage, and obligation cross-chain integrity. Coherently re-signed storage must not defeat independent causal-chain verification.
16. Design real external delivery and performed-action records as separate future capabilities. Do not infer delivery or action from an M1 audience-response record, Actor output, authorization, or freeze.
17. Execute the autonomous web-product-studio task-marketplace vertical slice only after identity, embodiment, relationships, and the worker/tool gateway are reliable.
18. Preserve the broader Fibre ambition—social, familial, cultural, institutional, developmental, and economic life—while requiring each new layer to pass persistence, provenance, authorization, replay, adversarial-evidence, causal-differentiation, and human-inspection standards.
