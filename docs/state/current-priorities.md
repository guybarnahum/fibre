# Current priorities

1. Preserve the fully closed deterministic M1 Persistent Thread Round Trip as historical executable evidence, while treating the canonical pre-M2 service merged in PR #31 as the live appraisal/runtime baseline. Historical M1 evidence must not be silently inherited as proof of semantic individuality or Development on the canonical service.
2. Execute the accepted [`Pre-M2 bridge plan`](../validation/pre-m2-bridge-plan.md) and refined [`M2 PR plan`](../validation/m2-pr-plan.md) in order. #33 and #34 are **EARNED / SEALED**. **#35 Structured Obligation v1 is MERGED / REVIEWED, the direct no-PR interrupted-compelled-history follow-up is LANDED, and #36 M2 Identity & Embodiment Contract is now the active milestone.** After #36, proceed to #37 Thread Passport & Identity Provenance v1, #38 Lineage/Geography/Embodiment v1, #39 Identity Projection & Causal Consumption, and #40 M2 Standing Gate / closure. Post-M2 sequence begins with #41 Self-authored Development, #42 Reciprocal Relationships, and #43 Economic Consequence / M3 foundation.
3. Preserve #33 Semantic Guardian standing evidence and methodology without tuning against sealed v1-v4 results. Continue to defend against assistant-mode collapse, unsupported factors, provider/protocol failure being mistaken for a Thread decision, persistent self-conditioning, caller-authored private state selection, and semantic-state absence being treated as neutral evidence.
4. Preserve **Semantic State v0** as defined by [`emotions-and-needs.md`](../concepts/emotions-and-needs.md): natural-language-first state; closed domains with registered extensible dimensions; evidence for every persisted change; `asOf`, supersession, provenance, staleness, restricted visibility, descriptive-not-instructional validation, and Fibre-owned bounded state selection. Requester-specific relationship attitudes remain **Semantic Relationship State v0**.
5. Preserve #34's earned but deliberately limited Development `0 -> 1` claim: a Fibre-owned durable record formed through an earlier canonical episode survived restart and causally changed a later appraisal under exact one-memory withholding. Do **not** silently reinterpret this as rich self-authored learning. The current memory is deterministic episode/request-derived description; Thread-authored reflection, experience-derived self-model/state change, adverse/low-dignity experience memory, and repeated cross-episode learning remain explicit extension targets.
6. Preserve #35's Structured Obligation authority invariants from [`structured-obligations-v1.md`](../architecture/structured-obligations-v1.md): **caller nomination is attention, never authority; Fibre determines applicability; compulsion never rewrites consent; completed one-shot compelled participation must leave an atomic durable discharge consequence.** Historical applicability is evidence, not a perpetual capability.
7. Treat merged #35 A-F plus the hostile-review closure as one integrity chain: **A)** domain/schema/migration + legacy-spend tombstones; **B)** ObligationStore/current-revision integrity; **C)** persisted Fibre applicability; **D)** runtime authorization cutover; **E)** atomic freeze/discharge cutover; **F)** read-only private/admin inspection; **review closure)** bidirectional discharge evidence, policy re-derivation, truthful structured compulsion presentation, structured freeze integrity, exact public Thread shape, bounded HTTP conflicts, and `governing_authority_withdrawn` interrupted-runtime closure. Preserve the direct follow-up invariant that an executed compelled/Guardian-passed episode whose authority was already withdrawn cannot disappear through lease expiry: protect it from lazy sweep, append a public-safe `COMPELLED_EPISODE_INTERRUPTED` life event, and keep full withdrawal evidence privately enumerable and verifiable.
8. Never auto-promote `currentState.unresolvedIntentions` into active obligations. The legacy field includes personal unfinished intentions and cannot establish commitment authority. Preserve already-consumed exact legacy references as deterministic tombstones so pre-migration spent authority cannot resurrect. Legitimate active obligations require explicit authoritative classification.
9. Preserve F's inspection boundary: live obligation inspection is private-token, loopback-only, GET-only and `no-store`; the verifier opens the database read-only with SQLite `query_only`; it performs no repair; public Thread/event/health routes expose no private obligation evidence. The same cross-chain verifier is available administratively through `npm run inspect:obligations`.
10. Treat [`M2 Identity & Embodiment Contract`](../architecture/m2-identity-embodiment-contract.md) as the active design authority for #36. Identity must be a **layered, provenance-bearing life**, not one biography/profile blob: distinguish constitutive facts, inherited disposition, upbringing/culture, geography, intellectual formation, lineage, roles, embodiment, lived episodes, external attribution, and current self-authored interpretation. Preserve contradictions and reinterpretation through append-only/superseding history rather than destructive rewriting.
11. Design #37-#40 so Fibre can prove **stable personality through decisions**, not merely different context text. For a named relevant identity/history cause, require the standing discipline from the Thread differential gate: same request fingerprint; Fibre-owned identity selection; exact evidence refs; symmetric swap; paraphrase invariance; contradiction sensitivity; ablation; restart; and, for non-deterministic cognition, repeated within-Thread stability with inter-Thread separation greater than intra-Thread variation. A strong M2 proof should also test continuity of identity-grounded judgment across a genuine cognition/model replacement when compatible runtimes are available.
12. Keep culture, lineage, gender, nationality, appearance, accent, and other identity labels from becoming stereotype shortcuts. Cultural meaning must come from lived biography and explicit Thread relationship to that formation. Embodiment may affect self-model, social interaction, or presentation only through a declared legitimate consumer; portrait/voice swaps alone may not manufacture competence, values, temperament, or willingness.
13. Preserve self-authorship without rewriting history. A Thread may affirm, reject, dispute, or reinterpret inherited, cultural, Echo-source, or externally attributed identity; the historical source remains inspectable while the current self-authored meaning may supersede prior interpretation. Origin influences a Thread but does not own its mature future identity.
14. Treat durable, targeted, evidence-backed relationship attitudes introduced through Semantic State v0 as **Semantic Relationship State v0**, the first layer of a relationship aggregate. Do not award broader social/relationship credit until richer reciprocal relationship mechanisms change later appraisal or future possibility. Keep the broader relationship service deferred: reciprocal/shared relationship structures, commitments/expectations, repair processes, relationship-specific permissions, family/social roles, and richer relational mechanisms remain later work.
15. Keep the general worker/tool/model gateway **deferred** during M2 identity work. A narrowly scoped Guardian model adapter is permitted for appraisal only; Actor remains deterministic and tool/network incapable, and Goal Guardian remains an audit layer rather than a production sandbox. Identity continuity must belong to the Thread rather than become fused to one cognition implementation.
16. Keep the API-backed Thread Editor non-authoritative. Every editor API request requires its per-run credential; the editor may inspect existing public/private lifecycle and expression records and request previews, but may not seed, accept commands, issue participation authority, write disclosure or response records, acquire runtime, run workers, freeze, abandon, repair, mutate obligations, or become an identity-authoring bypass. M2 may expand readable identity inspection only with the same explicit authority/privacy separation.
17. Keep command acceptance and projection repair behind administrative authority. Command preview remains public and non-mutating; preview identity is derivable from its public receipt, so the admin token—not redaction—is the authority boundary.
18. Preserve the completed hostile-review proofs from #35 and the direct history follow-up: a discharged revision requires exactly one discharge witness; applicability is independently re-derived; structured compulsion remains human-visible without legacy prose refs; live authority cannot be closed as withdrawn; stale authority after execution closes idempotently without consumption/discharge; qualified interrupted episodes remain closable after lease expiry and are replayable/enumerable Thread history. Continue broader adversarial hardening afterward; coherently re-signed storage must not defeat independent causal-chain verification.
19. Design real external delivery and performed-action records as separate future capabilities. Do not infer delivery or action from an M1 audience-response record, Actor output, authorization, or freeze.
20. Execute the autonomous web-product-studio task-marketplace vertical slice only after identity, embodiment, relationships, and the worker/tool gateway are reliable.
21. Preserve the broader Fibre ambition—social, familial, cultural, institutional, developmental, and economic life—while requiring each new layer to pass persistence, provenance, authorization, replay, adversarial-evidence, causal-differentiation, and human-inspection standards. M2 should make a Thread feel like one continuing person whose detailed history can explain characteristic decisions, not a workflow agent wearing a richer persona.
