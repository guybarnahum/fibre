# Current priorities

1. **Close the #39 Pre-G seam before creating final-cohort people.** Gate C, Gate D and Gate F are CLEAR. Stages 0-6 are complete; Stage 7 documentation/context reconciliation is implemented and awaiting local verification. Then complete Stage 8 repository hygiene and Stage 9 narrow readiness review. Until the seam closes, create no final G WorldSpecs/genomes, run no cohort familiarity probe, and make no G/H model call.

2. **Preserve the experiment-before-subject boundary.** Slice G must freeze five fresh WorldSpecs, then genomes/treatment assignment, EventStructurePool v2, cognition configuration, raters, diagnostics, uncertainty/threshold interpretation and the exact CLEAR/HOLD/REDESIGN rule **before** final lives are generated. The test exists before the people.

3. **Preserve #39's three-pass authority split.** Pass A writes observable history and is genome blind. Pass B forms selective autobiography and may receive only the frozen life/genome treatment. Pass C forms/reinterprets durable meaning, is one-memory scoped and genome blind. Fibre-computed semantic need conclusions and mechanical-condition values remain absent from all three Genesis cognition passes.

4. **Preserve history, memory and meaning as distinct authorities.** Events may be forgotten; memories may remain without durable meaning; meaning may be ambivalent, mistaken, unresolved, revised, or survive a later echo unchanged. Do not turn funnel or citation-share characterization into admission quotas.

5. **Protect rich-life generation from backwards personality authoring.** Richness means experiential fertility. Do not add minimum books, people, places, trauma, adversity, novelty or maturity milestones merely to make biographies look differentiated. Keep world affordances broad, let historical continuity constrain facts without becoming a literary theme template, and preserve weak lives as evidence.

6. **Preserve source/origin integrity.** A living identifiable source requires documented Echo consent; Homage requires a deceased or fictional subject. Public availability is not consent. Source biography never becomes Thread autobiography by implication. Thread-parent/Echo/Homage/fork remain origin-integrity paths, not separate personality-authoring compilers.

7. **Keep scientific evidence lifecycle explicit.** `npm test` is the active regression/operator suite; `test:repro` preserves retained proof/experiment instruments; `test:all` is the complete retained envelope. A historical executable under `tools/repro/` remains reproducibility evidence, not current production authority. Never delete a failed/burned result because later evidence is cleaner.

8. **Keep Fibre's causal claims conservative.** The Whole-Person checkpoint remains 15/26. #39 gives a Thread a particular prior life but earns no standing. #40 must make bounded selected identity/history causally matter; #41 must prove stable attributable non-interchangeability under repeated trials and causal substitutions.

9. **Close the current two-world cognition seam in #40 rather than hiding it in #39.** The rich #37/#38/#39 ledgers exist, but the live Guardian still substantially consumes legacy identity/trait/memory surfaces. #40 owns Fibre-side relevance/privacy selection, the bounded Identity Context Capsule, short model-visible handles with exact Fibre-side provenance, and standing-grade substitution controls. Caller nomination must not become private identity/history selection authority.

10. **Require behavioral consequence for functional personhood claims.** Storage, schema validity, provenance, prompt inclusion, UI display and context difference are valuable substrate but are not causal individuality. When a field is claimed functional, name the Thread-owned semantic input and the judgment, relationship, action, resource or future-state consequence it can change.

11. **Keep mechanical/substrate causality separate from Thread semantic authority.** Future mechanical conditions may be versioned, replayable triggers/modulators, but their values/labels may not be rendered as semantic need/emotion/meaning/self-knowledge or cited to earn identity/interiority standing. A condition-triggered episode may notice/reflect/form intention but may not perform protected action in the same episode.

12. **Preserve natural-language-first identity and inheritance.** The symbolic genome is ordered textual loci with exact provenance, deterministic crossover and explicit mutation. Numeric controls may measure or modulate machinery; they are not a second hidden heritable personality authority. Culture, nationality, lineage, gender, appearance, accent and other labels may shape lived affordances but may not mechanically imply morality, competence, politics, dignity or willingness.

13. **Preserve Thread self-authorship and historical integrity.** A Thread may later affirm, reinterpret or reject inherited/cultural/source identity without rewriting where it came from. Current self-account may fail to explain durable history. Fibre must preserve that residue rather than editing the past into coherence.

14. **Keep current authority and historical evidence distinct in documentation and AI context.** Core context should emphasize current doctrine/state/active M2 contracts. Full context may include failed/sealed Guardian/history/Genesis development evidence. Moving historical material out of `core` must never erase it from repository or context coverage.

15. **After #39, proceed in order:** #40 Identity Projection & Causal Consumption; #41 M2 Standing Gate; #42 Self-authored Development; #43 Reciprocal Relationships; #44 Economic Consequence/M3 foundation. Do not collapse those into one “identity implementation” because each closes a distinct personhood risk.

16. **Keep the broader ambition visible.** Model/runtime replacement, endogenous motivation, reciprocal social life, economic constraint/opportunity, task markets, family/reproduction, institutions and broader society remain deferred extension paths, not rejected capabilities.
