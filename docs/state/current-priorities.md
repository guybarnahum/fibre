# Current priorities

1. Preserve the fully closed deterministic M1 Persistent Thread Round Trip as historical executable evidence, while treating the canonical pre-M2 service merged in PR #31 as the live appraisal/runtime baseline. Historical M1 evidence must not be silently inherited as proof of semantic individuality or Development on the canonical service.
2. Execute the accepted [`Pre-M2 bridge plan`](../validation/pre-m2-bridge-plan.md) in order: **#32 bridge-plan synchronization -> #33 Semantic Guardian -> #34 History bends judgment -> #35 Structured Obligation v1 -> #36 M2 contract -> M2 implementation**. The earlier work order that put structured obligations or M2 contract work ahead of the semantic/developmental proofs is superseded.
3. For #33, make refusal reachable and attributable rather than optimizing only for willing acceptance. Defend first against assistant-mode collapse, held-out prompt/evaluation overfitting, hallucinated unsupported factors, non-deterministic replay, and accidental obligation use on the newly live aligned-accept path. Model failure must not be persisted as the Thread's judgment.
4. For #34, prove a real closed developmental loop on the canonical socket: a substantive earlier episode becomes freeze-validated durable history, survives restart, is resolved into later cognition, and changes a later appraisal or choice under a counterfactual that removes the causal memory. Memory must describe what happened rather than encode a future instruction.
5. For #35, replace M1 unresolved-intention strings with structured obligation records carrying stable identity, issuer/parties, scope, terms, expiry, recurrence, satisfaction criteria, discharge history, provenance, visibility classification, and request-bound applicability evidence. **Fibre must determine applicability; a caller may nominate but may not make an obligation governing by citation.** Record applicability author, policy, and version. Preserve the invariant that pre-migration spent obligations remain spent.
6. Define the M2 Identity and Embodiment contract in #36 only after #33 and #34 have demonstrated what semantic cognition and developmental history actually consume. The contract covers durable Thread passport, portrait and voice provenance, geography timeline, family/lineage representation, privacy boundaries, mutation/version rules, self-authorship, and falsifiable behavioral acceptance scenarios. Different compiled identity context alone is not M2 closure.
7. Keep the durable relationship aggregate/service **deferred** during the pre-M2 bridge. Until it exists, semantic cognition must report unsupported relational meaning as unresolved rather than hallucinating it.
8. Keep the general worker/tool/model gateway **deferred** during the pre-M2 bridge. A narrowly scoped Guardian model adapter is permitted for appraisal only; Actor remains deterministic and tool/network incapable, and Goal Guardian remains an audit layer rather than a production sandbox.
9. Keep the API-backed Thread Editor non-authoritative. Every editor API request requires its per-run credential; the editor may inspect public/private lifecycle and expression records and request previews, but may not seed, accept commands, issue participation authority, write disclosure or response records, acquire runtime, run workers, freeze, abandon, repair, or mutate obligations.
10. Keep command acceptance and projection repair behind administrative authority. Command preview remains public and non-mutating; preview identity is derivable from its public receipt, so the admin token—not redaction—is the authority boundary.
11. Continue targeted adversarial hardening, especially mutation baselines, crash/concurrency cases, cross-connection races, source-integrity reporting, model-evaluation holdout discipline, and any new authority path added by aligned semantic acceptance or structured obligations.
12. Design real external delivery and performed-action records as separate future capabilities. Do not infer delivery or action from an M1 audience-response record, Actor output, authorization, or freeze.
13. Execute the autonomous web-product-studio task-marketplace vertical slice only after identity, embodiment, relationships, and the worker/tool gateway are reliable.
14. Preserve the broader Fibre ambition—social, familial, cultural, institutional, developmental, and economic life—while requiring each new layer to pass persistence, provenance, authorization, replay, adversarial-evidence, causal-differentiation, and human-inspection standards.
