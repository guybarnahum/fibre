# World compute optimization — proposed plan

## Aim

Spend cognition only where it can help a persistent Thread live a richer, more causally individual life. World deadlines, mechanical plan-position changes and indexed due-work discovery should remain inexpensive deterministic state operations. Optimization is a reduction in **wasted inference and redundant work**, not a smaller ambition, shorter lived history, fewer legitimate encounters or a quota on interesting experiences.

Measure before and after with [World cost accounting](world-cost-accounting.md); do not infer savings from fewer lines of code or assume a model call is wasteful simply because it concludes `no_change` or `not_noticed`.

## Candidate improvements, in order of evidence

1. **Plan-horizon renewal first.** The current 12-hour plan's boundary may wake at `horizonEnd + 1 ms`. Inspect the actual `LivedNow.ensure`/catch-up path to determine whether the one-millisecond uncovered gap invokes an unnecessary *retrospective* plan in addition to the next *forward* plan. This is a **hypothesis, not a diagnosed live defect**. If confirmed, renew the next forward plan from the exact admitted horizon boundary without inventing retrospective history for negligible elapsed time. Preserve long-dormancy catch-up and honest retrospective provenance. Expected benefit: eliminate an otherwise duplicated model-backed plan renewal, not eliminate legitimate future planning.
2. **Material regulation only.** Confirm whether stop updates, same-scene refreshes, transit phases and short-lived nonchanges are already filtered through the deterministic intrinsic-regulation attention gate. Only fix an observed duplicate or ineffective prefilter. The Thread still decides what a genuinely material pressure means; do not substitute a cheap mechanical emotional verdict.
3. **World opportunity and attention efficiency.** Count `no_change`, changed, potential-witness, `noticed`, and `not_noticed` paths separately. Where objective World evidence can cheaply establish *no compatible perception opportunity*, skip inference. Otherwise let bounded authoring and independent attention remain genuinely open: do not suppress ordinary events through quotas, text heuristics, artificial scarcity, site popularity thresholds or a universal weather tick.
4. **Reuse admission, not hallucinated state.** On retries reuse durable plan/Story/attention decisions and canonical model request IDs rather than re-sampling already-admitted facts. Inspect actual provider retry rates, prompt size, evidence retrieval and cached-input possibilities before altering inference profiles. Preserve individualized semantic context and authored meaning, not just condensed labels, and never truncate private memories solely to hit a budget target.
5. **No global optimization sweep.** Apply changes at earned plan renewal, World event, personal appraisal or admitted message frontiers. No population scans, speculative perpetual cognition or precomputing whole days for inactive Threads.

## Measurement and stopping rules

Track **model calls and input/output tokens per successfully admitted Flight Plan horizon, deterministic stop advance, environmental consideration, noticed Experience and delayed consolidation**, plus retries and elapsed time. Also report the legitimate quiet outcomes, so reducing calls cannot be mistaken for an improvement if Fibre stopped noticing the World.

Acceptance for the initial improvement is one natural/controlled plan-renewal proof: the admitted Flight Plan advances continuously across the horizon without a spurious tiny retrospective plan, duplicated model invocation, missed next boundary or history rewrite; the number of planning model calls is demonstrably lower than the measured baseline. A similarly small semantic proof is required for any later regulation/opportunity change.

**Timing:** inspect and optimize **after** the scheduled E7.5 autonomous boundary has been observed; do not risk invalidating that staging experiment with a premature deployment. Prefer a focused renewal fix, if measured, before reworking World reconciliation scaling. Do not introduce caching, call-rate knobs or new schedulers absent a demonstrated Fibre benefit.
