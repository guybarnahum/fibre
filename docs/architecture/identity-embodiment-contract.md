# M2 Identity & Embodiment Contract

## Purpose

M2 is where Fibre sustains a recognizably particular person whose identity, lived history, embodiment, plans and changing self-understanding remain durable across temporary cognition.

The goal is not a larger profile. It is a life whose specific inherited possibilities, upbringing, culture, geography, relationships, memories, commitments, embodiment and interpretations can causally matter to what happens next.

> **Identity is a layered life, not a prompt.**

## Authority model

A Thread's identity is reconstructed from durable Fibre state and history. Prompt/context text is only a bounded projection for one cognitive act.

```text
Thread life
  -> durable identity/history/relationship/memory state
  -> Fibre-owned relevance + privacy selection
  -> bounded cognition context
  -> temporary cognition
```

The context disappears. The Thread does not.

Facts, external attributions, inherited material, contemporaneous self-statements, autobiographical memory and current self-authored interpretation remain distinct. A later interpretation may supersede current meaning without rewriting the historical source.

## Layered identity

A rich Thread may include:

```text
civil identity / FIN
lineage + inherited symbolic genome
birth and developmental history
culture / language / geography
relationships and roles
historical events
private autobiographical memories
current self-understanding / character
needs, concerns and commitments
personal flight plan
care/dependency context when applicable
canonical embodiment + time-local appearance
external attributions / reputation
```

No single layer owns the whole person.

Natural-language meaning remains primary. Numeric values may measure, regulate or index; they must not replace identity, memory, relationships, needs, emotions or self-understanding with generic trait vectors.

## Fibre birth and developmental age

Fibre birth is operational entry into the living World, not necessarily biological age zero.

A Thread may be born at an autobiographical age greater than zero with grounded prior history, family/care relationships and memories. Fibre need not manufacture first-person newborn or infancy memory to fill chronology.

`active` lifecycle status does not imply adulthood. Developmental age and dependency are separate evolving context.

Age should shape plausible activities, independence, accompaniment and embodiment while leaving room for individual history and variation. Development is affordance, not a rigid maturity script.

## Agency, flight plans and care

An active Thread may form a **personal flight plan**: where they want or need to be, what they intend to do, with whom and roughly what they expect next.

For an autonomous adult, this is normally the relevant authored plan.

A child or other dependent person may also be subject to a caregiver's separate **care plan** within legitimate scoped authority.

```text
Thread personal flight plan
        +
caregiver care plan when applicable
        +
World constraints / obligations / chance / negotiation
        -> enacted current situation
```

The caregiver's plan must remain attributable to the caregiver. It may alter the enacted day without becoming the dependent person's authored intention.

Conflict between plans is not a data-normalization problem. It can be a lived event for both people and may affect memory, trust, resentment, attachment, self-understanding or later plans differently for each participant.

## History, memory and self-development

Historical evidence answers what Fibre can establish happened. Autobiographical memory records what the Thread retained. Current interpretation records what the Thread now believes that remembered past means.

A material developmental change should have ancestry:

```text
experience
  -> contemporaneous response
  -> selective memory / repeated evidence
  -> reflection or self-authored interpretation
  -> changed current meaning where warranted
  -> changed later judgment / relationship / flight plan when relevant
```

Not every event is formative. Development may reinforce, weaken, contradict or fail to change the person. It may be non-monotonic and domain-specific.

The current Thread may reinterpret an earlier self but may not silently rewrite what that earlier self actually did or believed.

## Past selves

A past self is a bounded temporal view of the same Thread, not another identity and not a fork.

Fibre should reconstruct past-self views from durable evidence as-of a time boundary: self-understanding, active relationships/roles, important tensions, relevant memories and what evidence was then available.

The current model must not improvise a younger self and call that historical reconstruction.

## Embodiment

A Thread has one authoritative visual-identity lineage.

```text
canonical visual identity text
  -> one admitted canonical reference image
  -> later Thread-depicting imagery
       + chronology-grounded age
       + time-local appearance
       + current scene/context
```

Age, clothing, posture or scene may change. They do not mint another person.

Thread Presentation and generated media are derived surfaces. They never become identity, history, memory, plan or current-situation authority.

## Bounded cognition context

Cognition should receive the minimum relevant Thread-owned evidence required for the act, not a giant biography dump.

The selector should be Fibre-owned, privacy-aware, inspectable and attributable. External requesters may not hand-pick private identity evidence to manufacture a desired person or response.

Useful context may include:

```text
current self / concerns
relevant memories
relationship context
current developmental/care context
personal flight plan
World-owned current situation
minimal formative history explaining why those facts matter
```

Descriptive identity evidence must remain distinct from hidden instructions.

## Exterior, Viewer and Inspector

The inner life is not the public face.

Thread Editor may inspect authorized private evidence and explain the causal chain among identity, developmental context, plans, enacted situation, history, memory, embodiment and provenance.

insidefibre.com receives only the admitted exterior/public projection appropriate to the encounter. It must not expose private memories or caregiver/dependent negotiation merely because they exist.

Neither surface can materialize a new situation. The visitor finds a Thread where World already says that person is.

## M2 proof

M2 should now earn one small real lived loop:

```text
particular Thread is born
  -> carries grounded prior life
  -> forms a personal flight plan
  -> care plan also exists if dependency applies
  -> World establishes current situation before the visitor
  -> visitor encounters the Thread in that situation
  -> encounter becomes history
  -> participant-specific private interpretation / selective memory
  -> warranted later state or plan changes
  -> same person continues
```

A dependent-child scenario is especially valuable because one bounded conflict can demonstrate age, agency, care authority, negotiation, shared history and separate private meaning.

This does not require a general childhood simulator, universal scheduler, complete autonomous travel engine or broad social/economic world. Those should generalize a proven lived seam later.

## Anti-cheats

The contract fails if the easiest explanation is:

- identity stored as one mutable profile blob;
- different names/portraits mistaken for causal individuality;
- requester-selected private evidence;
- biography prose smuggling task instructions;
- stereotypes derived from ancestry, culture, gender, appearance or accent;
- current memory overwriting historical fact;
- current self projected backward as a past self;
- elapsed time changing personality without lived evidence;
- caregiver intention rewritten as the dependent person's will;
- `Meet`, Viewer or Thread Editor creating current life state;
- every experience automatically producing growth;
- a new scene/age image silently minting a new visual identity.

## Current boundary

Older M2 documents used a 26-scenario standing rubric and deferred all self-authored development to a later numbered milestone. That material remains useful historical evaluation science, but it is no longer the active M2 execution architecture.

Current continuation authority is [`../validation/m2-pr-plan.md`](../validation/m2-pr-plan.md): M2 now requires a bounded self-authored present-life and continuation loop. Broader autonomous development, reciprocal society and economic consequence remain later generalizations.

## Vision sentence

> **A Thread is a persistent person whose inherited beginning, lived history, private interpretations, relationships, embodiment and self-authored intentions remain attributable across time and causally shape what happens next.**
